WORKSTREAM hmgcs: REPORT (2026-11-20)
=====================================
BOTTOM LINE
- Lead 1: SUPPORTED as an HMGS-type condensing enzyme; mevalonate-pathway role UNVERIFIED (the next step, HMG-CoA
  reductase, HMGCR, is absent from all 17 genomes). The 580-aa protein is a long-form HMG-CoA synthase (bacterial/
  eukaryotic type), no fused partner, Glu-Cys-His triad at the curated positions, TM 0.91-0.92 to E. faecalis HMGS 1YSL;
  as a homodimer it binds acetyl-CoA at the Cys.
- The 17 are sub-threshold members of a 288-gene Micrarchaeota family that Pfam names at GA in 155 members; a 78-aa
  insertion splits the Pfam C domain into two halves under the domain GA. 13/17 calls are embedding-only: a clean
  methods example with a routine family label.
- Biology: the family replaces the canonical archaeal HMGCS (3 genomes carry both vs 51 expected, Fisher p = 4e-24) and
  runs without the archaeal DUF35 scaffold. Micrarchaeum_A keeps it while HMGCR is absent in 44/44 genus genomes, with
  the Thermoplasma-type mevalonate-3-kinase route present downstream.
- DPANN-wide, a blank HMGS step is mostly a threshold artifact: 557/1,012 HMGS-blank lipid genomes carry a ~348-aa
  protein scoring 205 bits against KOfam K01641 (threshold 282) that Pfam labels ACP_syn_III_C. HMGCR-blank genomes
  show zero sub-threshold reductase hits, so that absence is real.

FRAME-CHALLENGE GATE (trigger 3: HMGCR absent where the premise "lead fills a pathway gap" needs it)
Pinned anomalies: (1) HMGCR 0/17 and 0/44 in the genus, downstream mevalonate-3-kinase route present; (2) long form
  exclusive of canonical HMGCS yet with HMGCR in JAJYXL01 26/33, JAKARM01 17/18, JAKATK01 13/13; (3) Tyr339 at the
  HMG-CoA-binding Lys; (4) no pathway genes near the lead. Hypotheses: (a) host supplies mevalonate; (b) neofunctionalized
  condensing enzyme; (c) non-homologous HMGCR; (d) HMG-CoA consumed by another route.
Tests run: (b), proposed by the fresh-context critic: pocket residues by genus vs HMGCR status give one pocket family-wide
  (Tyr 100% in HMGCR-carrying JAKARM01, JAKATK01 and in HMGCR-blank Micrarchaeum_A 0/33; triad and N/S invariant in
  270/270), so (b) is disfavored and in HMGCR genera the long form is the only HMGS upstream of HMGCR. (d): HMGCL and
  leucine-catabolism KOs 0/17. (c): profiling in Micrarchaeaceae (125 HMGCR+ vs 139 HMGCR- mevalonate-using genomes)
  found no reductase-like family; top enriched family LacAB_rpiB (78% vs 15%; rpib workstream's family, UNVERIFIED link).
  - (a) stays open; it needs wet-lab or host-symbiont data.
