FAILURE ANALYSIS
- Ribosomal runs: with lists, the adaptive distance costs 45 runs against window 3 (odd 781 -> 758); with families it
  cost none (part 4: -4 against part-1 L4b). 16% of Z-WA's bonded boundaries are bonded only by pairs 4-8 apart. The
  per-run mechanism was not diagnosed here; joining runs to conserved flanks through far list pairs is the candidate
  (UNVERIFIED; the part-1 ribosomal diagnosis code can classify the 1,497-run output's failures directly).
- Spacedust: the union coverage of long regions is near the engine's (.85 vs .89 for 11+ genes) and F1 within .03, but
  the nested L4b+ output carries a control of .571; the engine's union of readings has .566. The L1 runs carry .425 and
  match the engine on excess.
- CRISPR-Cas: list identity keeps its margin at the adaptive distance (+.012; part-2 Z +.010), so the trade against
  family WA (-.019) is CRISPR-Cas for ribosomal runs.

CAVEATS
- The selection rule ranks odd conditions before resolved runs; Z-WA L4b+ met all three odd conditions with odd resolved
  758 < DH odd 769 (the odd ribosomal condition is >= 705), so the rule could select an output that misses the even
  ribosomal bar.
- DPANN masks were rebuilt from the stored kNN through vl.emb_lists (16.7 min of the 36 min); a stored capcos array
  would remove most of it.
- Spacedust kNN is exact (part 3), DPANN's is HNSW (vocab_lists); both feed only masks and cohesion.
