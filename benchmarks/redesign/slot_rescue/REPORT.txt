ANSWER
1. The slot call rescues 81.5% of the verified dark core genes with embedding lists and 73.5% with alignment lists, at a
   0.13-0.18% false-rescue rate on 8,474 matched control ORFs. On the 2,332 proteins with neither a Pfam nor the
   right-family KO the rates are 77.1% and 67.1%; embedding lists add +10.0 [+8.7, +11.5] points there. The predeclared
   'works' criterion (slot or combined >= 50% at <= 2% control false rescue) is met.
2. The top-10 list call rescues about half (51.8% Z, 47.3% A). Its failures are mostly empty votes: in 989 of 1,399 Z
   failures the ten nearest partners carry no Pfam or KO at all: the closest relatives are unannotated in their own
   genomes too. The combined call as predeclared (top 10 of the slot-restricted list) adds nothing (+0.3 points): the
   ten nearest partners already sit in the slot.
3. POST HOC (not predeclared; results/posthoc_table.tsv): a majority vote over the whole list without any positional
   filter rescues exactly as many verified genes as the slot call (Z 2,368 vs 2,368, 2 differ each way; A 2,183 vs
   2,134), but names a core gene for 169 of 8,474 controls (1.99%; 92 of them Ribosomal_L40e, then KO K02928, which
   Sharur maps to ribosomal_protein, Ribosomal_L32e, Ribosomal_L15e) against 11 (0.13%) for the slot call. The rescue power comes from list depth (a
   right-family partner sits somewhere in the Z list for 2,378 of 2,904); the slot supplies the specificity, a 15-fold
   cut in false core names. The slot-restricted whole list without bond or locus gating gives 81.6% at 0.22%.
4. The predicted confusion of short basic proteins is small under these calls: wrong family of the same broad class in
   4-8 of 2,904 verified genes per route (wrong other family 30-57). It appears in the whole-list vote on controls
   (above), where short ribosomal-protein families dominate the false names.
5. Detection-limited sets. eL39 slot ORFs: slot Z 98.0% against 17.2% for the Z list and 40.4% for slot A (45 of 99 have
   an empty alignment list). uL29 and uL15 slot ORFs: 83-100% by lists and slot Z; slot A reaches 18.9% on uL29 (39 of
   53 have no alignment bond). DPANN-profile rescues (1,191): slot 92% on both arms against 30-32% for lists; the eL39
   profile set is 98.8% by slot and 4.6% / 16.6% by lists. For DL_slot sets the slot call is partly circular (the truth
   was defined by position); their list calls are the independent test.
6. Negatives: 29 of 2,174 instances are named the lead's X by the slot Z call, 20 of them in L27 (VIT1 slot beside
   DUF211: 20 of 22 named VIT1 by every route, while the study scored 0 of its sample against the VIT1 profile; UNVERIFIED
   either way), 5 in L13 (Oxidored_q4) and 4 in L05 (K06875). 389 of the 394 L05 instances that the study also lists under
   L02 receive the study's eL39 call. The other negatives stay unnamed or receive non-X names (L08: Gins51_C 124,
   Ribosomal_S13_N 51 by slot Z; L01: PepSY 48), reported here as method calls only, UNVERIFIED.

DEFINITIONS (predeclared.json)
- Lists: Z = ws_calib z >= 10.6 best gene per genome outside the own group (vl.z_lists); A = MMseqs2 aln__k1000 outside
  the own group, depth 1,000 (vl.aln_lists). Bonds: nodes/dpann_{Z,A}/bonds_obs.npz (tau 6). Loci: Z L4b+ theta .02,
  A L4b theta .02 (each arm's selected output).
- Votes: a partner votes for the right family when it carries any label of the study's X (Pfam members, proxy Pfams,
  KOs); otherwise one vote per Pfam / KO-proxy key. Call = the plurality key with >= 2 votes; ties -> no call.
- List call: top 10 of the gene's list. Slot call: gene inside a locus and bonded; vote over the best-ranked supporting
  partner of every genome (partners of the gene 1-3 positions from a partner of a bonded neighbor). Combined: top 10 of
  the list restricted to partners lying 1-3 positions from a partner of any gene within 3 positions.
- Controls: 3 per truth protein, same genome, no Pfam and no KO, length within max(5 aa, 15%), not at a contig end, no
  gene within 2 positions carrying a member of the screen's 1,812 conserved pairs; seed 20261120. 11,904 drawn (184
  truth proteins had < 3 eligible, 53 had none). A control is scored with its truth protein's X; false rescue = right
  family of the match or any CORE label (all lead_X / DL families and every label Sharur maps to ribosomal_protein).

DIFFERENCES FROM THE BRIEF / LIMITS
- Truth rates are against the study's lead-level calls carried to every listed protein; the study records no
  per-protein evidence, so some listed proteins of a lead may be something else.
- 'Combined' uses no truth flank (slot consistency against any gene within 3 positions) and votes the top 10, as
  predeclared; the whole-list version is the POST HOC row. The no-Pfam-no-KO stratum and the whole-list votes were
  defined after the predeclared numbers were read and are labeled.
- uL15's family Pfam (Ribosomal_L27A) is shared with eL18, so a partner annotated as eL18 counts as right.
- The slot call for DL_slot sets is partly circular; DL_profile truth is per-protein profile evidence (independent).
- Two login-node smoke runs (2 processes, 71 s and 140 s, 3.2 GB) exceeded the 30 s login guideline.
- No vault capture or CONTEXT.md update (the brief restricts writes to this folder).

STEP 5: A GENERAL SLOT-RESCUE PASS FOR SHARUR (sketch; nothing built)
  Inputs: per-gene embedding lists (ws_calib table, z >= 10.6, own group excluded), the round-0 list bonds (tau 6), stored
    Pfam / KO rows, protein_id -> gid. Alignment lists optional (they rescue 10 points fewer on genes without a Pfam or KO).
  Candidates: every bonded gene without a Pfam row (DPANN: 1,444,991 genes lack a Pfam; 34% of the matched control
    ORFs here are bonded, 85% of the verified truth genes).
  Call: vote over the best-ranked list partner of every genome that lies 1-3 genes from a partner of a bonded neighbor;
    >= 2 votes, plurality; keep the positional filter (the whole-list vote has the same recall at 15x the false core names).
  Output table: gene, called Pfam / KO, votes for and against, supporting genomes, bonded neighbors, max bond Lambda; label
    it a positional-homology call, a hypothesis ('consistent with'), never a curated caller result.
  Cost on DPANN (estimate from this run: 109,737 genes' lists in 6.8 s, 38k row-arms voted in 14 s on 24 CPUs): all
    3.59 M genes' Z lists ~4 min and ~13 GB int32; votes for ~1.4 M dark genes ~10 min; about 15 min of one 24-CPU job.
  Expected yield from this test: ~77-81% right on dark core genes, <= 0.2% false core names on matched dark ORFs.
