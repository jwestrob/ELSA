CHOICES FOR JACOB
- Complete lutA-I genomes are all Methylobacterium and all 80 are in every size: 9% of the large set (28% of the small).
  Option: cap a1 at a fixed number (for example 30, chosen by the same rank) to free room for other lineages.
- Background is thin by design: 56 c1 genomes over 187 orders of the class (one genome for each of the 80 largest
  orders lacking a selected family) and 18 c2 genomes outside the class. Option: raise c1 / c2 without touching the
  lut layers (one parameter each).
- The DPANN parity split is not stratified by layer: a3 split 82 tuning / 59 test, a4 154 / 187, c2 7 / 11. Option: keep
  the DPANN rule (comparability) or alternate genome_code parity within each layer (balanced halves).
- Lut-negative relatives (b1, b2) exclude genomes with any reverse-best lutH homolog (receptor-only genomes); they are
  negatives for the whole cluster, not just the transporter module.
- The selection prefers CheckM2 >= 90% complete / <= 5% contaminated genomes inside every stratum (85% of the large set);
  incomplete MAGs enter only where a stratum has no better genome.

METHODS
Candidates: the 23,811 genomes scanned in part 1 (Rhizobiales 5,752; rest of the class 15,721; 2,338 targeted outside
  the class). Lut status from part-1 main loci and reverse-best lutH counts (S0 negative 17,301; S1 complete 80; S2
  receptor + transporter 336; S3 receptor module 17; S4 split 1,924; S5 transporter only 3,055; S6 other 5; S7 receptor
  only 1,093).
Rank: quality tier (Q0 >= 90 / <= 5, Q1 >= 70 / <= 10, Q2) then SHA-256 of "17:<accession>". Layer rules v2 in
  testset/selection_rules.txt; code/13_select.py writes candidates.tsv, ladder.tsv (k = 1-12), selection_k*.tsv.
Package builder: code/14_build_testset.py (count | build SELECTION_TSV) reads the part-1 Parquet parts, so any size is
  one command; numbering by the DPANN rules; genomes.parquet carries layer, lut_status, n_main_loci, locus_components,
  source_scope, quality_tier, split. Ribosomal truth: code/15_ribosomal_truth.py (standing definition of
  ribosomal/code/ribosomal_check.py and PREDECLARE.txt, with markers from a direct Pfam GA search). Seal: code/16_seal.py
  (SHA-256 of each file and of the sorted path/hash list). Wrapper: code/testset.sbatch.
Embedding (copied from transfer_srvp_bac/code, paths adapted, recipe unchanged): embed_queue prep in the build job
  (341 shards, 1,362,898,600 tokens, 3,099 sequences > 2,046 aa, longest 21,533 aa); code/embed/embed_gpu.sbatch
  (partition gpu, H200 node excluded, -c 12, one worker, bf16, float32 residue mean, whole protein in one pass);
  code/embed/finalize_zca.sbatch (embed_queue finalize, zca10 whitening fitted on this set, products linked into
  package_k8/embeddings, manifest; the SR-VP describe step is dropped); code/embed/gate.sh on the login node.
