   negatives reach positive-level co-fold coordination in 7/16, matching the lanthanide project's 2026-11-09 finding).
3. The one unannotated lut component with a lanthanide role is LutD, already named LanD in the literature (Eu Kd 340 nM).
   Of 14 diverged LanD-family copies, 6 keep its site and 8 (CAIVPW01, Labrys, SHVS01) lose it (observation).
4. Observations without interpretation: LutC has a cupredoxin fold with no conserved His or Cys (no type-1 Cu site) and a
   conserved Glu-Glu-Asp triad (3 carboxylates, below the rule); orf7 has a LolA/LprG lipoprotein-carrier fold; DUF3280 a
   TolB-N/LpoB fold. orf6, LutG and LutI match no PDB fold.

ANOMALIES AND NEXT TEST (not run)
 Six periplasmic families track lut or xoxF tightly (matched OR 33 to >300), two carry growth phenotypes, none has a
 conserved carboxylate site: any lanthanide role they have works through something other than free-ion chelation by
 carboxylates. Orthogonal hypotheses: (a) a metallophore-Ln complex bound in a cavity (orf7 fold); (b) a protein relay
 with LanM/LanD/LutA (precedent LanD-LanM Kd 4 uM; fails criterion A); (c) envelope or transporter accessory roles
 (DUF3280 TolB-N/LpoB fold). Cheapest discriminating test: pairwise co-folds (ipTM) of LutC, LutG, DUF3280 and orf6 with
 LanM, LanD and LutA against non-cognate periplasmic partners (about 60 folds).
