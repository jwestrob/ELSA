ws_kinds: recurrence of kinds for genes that have no homologs (DPANN, 3,564 genomes, zca10 ESM++ small)
13 November 2026. Predeclared in predeclared.json (02:06:38 UTC, before any job). Post hoc items are labelled.

ANSWER (eight lines)
1. A training-free statistic carries the critic's signal and exceeds it: kind recurrence kappa = R - R0, R = distinct
   embedding-family pairs among consecutive gene pairs elsewhere whose members sit in the two genes' top-1,000
   whitened lists, R0 = the same count with gene order permuted within genomes. No training, taxonomy, annotation or
   alignment is needed to run it.
2. Critic benchmark, held-out orders, EVEN genomes, DefenseFinder vs no-gold: never-co-located pairs kappa 0.797
   [0.759, 0.838] vs critic 0.756 (+0.041 [+0.003, +0.079]); singleton-family pairs 0.843 vs 0.781 (+0.061
   [-0.042, +0.160]); all 0.837 vs 0.823. PMI 0.563 and 0.500. TXSScan never-seen trails the critic (-0.058).
3. Co-fold test (predeclared, SUPPORTED): high-kappa pairs the token engine cannot call pass criteria 1-3 in 26/300
   (8.7% [5.7, 12.4]); length- and genome-matched low-kappa uncalled pairs 2/300 (0.67% [0.08, 2.39]); partner swaps
   0/150 [0, 2.4]. Ratio 13, Fisher p 9e-7; 21/300 after the split-gene and zipping rules. This operational claim,
   "kappa finds interacting pairs the token engine cannot call", stands whatever the homology status of the support.
4. Homology (ws_calib lines, addition): the co-fold signal rides on support that is homologous on at least one side.
   Passers' support units: 21.7% homologous to the query on both sides, 70.4% on one side, 7.9% on neither; class-a
   pairs whose support is > 50% homology-free on both sides pass 1/149, those with <= 10% pass 22/93 (post hoc).
5. Where alignment gives nothing on one side the embedding supplies it: pairs with a chain without any DPANN homolog
   at >= 50% coverage pass 8/108 vs 0/168 (p 0.0005, post hoc); in all 8, 71-100% of support units are homologous to
   the query on at least one side, in 7 mostly on exactly one. What co-folds is known binding kinds whose folded
   genes lack Pfam hits: 17 toxin-antitoxin-like, 2 RNase P subunits (observed domains; UNVERIFIED as systems).
6. The signal is short complexes: class a passes 18/51 under 200 aa, 7/79 at 200-300, 1/170 at 300-450 (screen-1
   engine-called 8/20, 6/56, 7/101). Dark architectures (members unannotated everywhere) co-fold at 1/36.
7. Withheld test REFUTED (predeclared): every family of the curated type withheld drops AUROC below chance (0.432).
   With the query's own families AND all families homologous to them withheld (addition), pooled AUROC falls from
   0.788 to 0.595 (DefenseFinder 0.547, T4P 0.518, CRISPR subtypes 0.717): 33% of the above-chance signal survives
   (DefenseFinder 16%, T4P 5%, CRISPR 83%); it survives for rare defence types (AbiL, Kiwa, Hachiman, Gao_Mza 0.95-0.98).
8. "Kinds without homology" holds for a minority: the 14,211 family pairs of 390 architectures form 7,445 groups that
   differ by homology on at least one side but only 1,356 groups that share no homologous member (median 1 per
   architecture, 18 architectures with >= 10); 55.8% of class-a support units are homology-free on both sides and
   that part does not predict a co-fold. The embedding adds one-side kind matching anchored by homology.

==============================================================================================================
METHOD
==============================================================================================================
Statistic (kinds.py; kernel checked against a pure-python recount, 0 mismatches over 120 queries x 2 orders x 3
exclusion modes x 2 reference sets)
  Lists     N(x) = the 1,000 nearest unique sequences of x by zca10 cosine (stored HNSW top-1,000, recall@1000
            0.994) plus x's own sequence; a gene's list = every gene carrying one of them, minus genes of the
            query's genome (median 1,376 genes, max 2,995).
  Query     every consecutive gene pair (a, b) on a contig (3,353,826).
  Support   consecutive pairs (a', b') in other genomes with a' in N(a), b' in N(b), any orientation.
  Units     realisation unit = (f(a'), f(b')), f = Leiden community of the zca10 mutual-kNN graph (k 20, resolution
            3,000; 153,520 families; vectors only). R = number of distinct units.
  Null      gene order permuted within each genome, contig sizes kept, seeds 1000-1002; query and lists fixed;
            R0 = mean R over the three permutations.
  Score     kappa = R - R0. Selected on odd genomes among 16 predeclared variants (units F / FX, depth 30 / 100 /
            300 / 1,000, ratio / excess); depth dominates (never-seen AUROC 0.67 / 0.71 / 0.78 / 0.80 for F at
            30 / 100 / 300 / 1,000; FX at depth 30 is at chance, 0.52).
  Cost      one pass over all adjacencies 105-230 s on 24 CPUs; observed + 3 nulls about 15 min after the kNN.
  Equivalent soft view (not run): the expected partner vector of a's list compared with b; the count form keeps
            multimodal partner kinds, which a mean vector blurs.

Benchmark (bench_eval.py): the grammar critic's split (5 folds by GTDB order); for an adjacency in fold k the
reference genomes are the training side of split k (train + validation genomes), so kappa and the critic see the
same genomes. Critic AUROC reproduced exactly first (never_w3 0.767587, singleton 0.786264).

==============================================================================================================
5. WHAT THIS SAYS ABOUT THE CLAIM "EMBEDDINGS DO WORK ALIGNMENT CANNOT" (read with section 8)
==============================================================================================================
Over pairs the token engine cannot call, kappa finds partners that co-fold at 13x the matched-control rate and at
the engine-called rate under 300 aa. Most of what it counts is homology that MMseqs2 clustering split (section 8):
the co-folding pairs' support is homologous on at least one side for 92% of units. The embedding's own contribution
is twofold: it collects split homologs without an alignment step, and it matches an orphan side by kind when the
partner side is anchored by homology (8 co-folding pairs with an orphan chain). Recurrence carried by family pairs
homologous on neither side (dark architectures 0, 4, 13, 20 and the critic's pattern: 0/42 class-a pairs plus 0/8
critic pairs; class-a pairs with > 50% such support: 1/149) co-folds at the base rate. That is either no binary
interface (envelope, phage-structural or regulatory associations) or ESMFold2 single-sequence blindness above 300
aa; this screen cannot separate the two.

==============================================================================================================
6. CAVEATS
==============================================================================================================
- The co-fold judge favours short, tight heterodimers; pass rates fall with length in every class and screen.
- Class a's passes are concentrated (one architecture holds 12/26); the per-pair interval is the predeclared one,
  the architecture-cluster interval is wider ([2.2%, 20.7%]).
- "Unannotated" = no row in the package annotation flags for that gene; the passers' kinds carry Pfam domains on
  other members. "No homologs" = no MMseqs2 -s 7.5 hit at >= 50% coverage in DPANN; profile searches were not run.
- The split-gene rule fires whenever any fused homolog exists for a pair seen in one genome (kA_006).
- Lists exclude only the query's own genome; close relatives in other MAGs count, and the unit collapse is the
  only guard against descent (it is weaker for the embedding families than for MMseqs2 families, section 1).
- The class-b match fell back beyond the genome for 76 pairs (genus 33, family 25, order 17, phylum 1).
- The L2 control matches family-size deciles, not gene counts.

8.4 Claim at the level the evidence supports
 - Stands, independent of homology: kappa is a training-free statistic that finds interacting pairs among those the
   token engine cannot call (26/300 vs 2/300; 21/300 after refutation rules), and it beats the critic on never-seen
   defence pairs.
 - Supported in part: proteins with no homolog at >= 50% coverage are paired correctly (8/108 vs 0/168) through
   one-side kind matching: the partner side is homologous to the support, the orphan side is matched by kind.
 - Not supported as a source of interaction: recurrence carried by family pairs homologous on neither side. It is the
   majority of class-a support (55.8%) and of the dark architectures, and those pairs co-fold at the base rate (1/149).
 - Kinds without homology exist as a minority signal: 1,356 fully independent groups among 14,211 family pairs, and
   L1h keeps AUROC >= 0.8 for a set of rarer defence and CRISPR types; for toxin-antitoxin, RM and T4P pairs the kind
   signal is homology that the clustering split.
