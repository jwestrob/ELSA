DESIGN AND DEVIATIONS
- Exact stratified lists (Task 1b): for every unique sequence (2,547,528) and genome (3,564), the best match (maximum
  zca10 cosine over the genome's genes and the gene attaining it), by float16 matrix products on one RTX A5000 (job
  84862); stored at cosine >= 0.32 (4,283,508,730 entries; median 1,579 genomes per sequence). A gene's list = its
  sequence's entries in genomes outside its genus; level = rank of the genome among the genus's genomes by cosine
  (0 = the genus's best match). Cheap version (Task 1a, source kn): the same per-genome bests inside the stored
  top-2,000 unique kNN.
- Variants: global cosine floor f in {0.32, 0.45, 0.60} x per-genus cap m in {1, 3, all}: a supporting pair (a', b')
  in genome G (a' = a's entry, b' = b's entry, a' != b', within 3 genes on one contig, both >= f) counts for G's genus
  only if min(level a', level b') < m ("best match per genus / best few"). Statistics: count L (genera other than own
  with a supporting pair; engine convention L + 1), and rank-weighted W_r = sum over genera of exp(-(t - 1)/r),
  r in {100, 1000}, with t = 1 + N_genes x P(cos >= c over all pairs) of the genus's best supporting pair (the cosine's
  expected position in a global ranked list). Same null (within-genome gene-order shuffle, seeds 1000-1002, every
  order-dependent step after it), same instance-level rule selection (FDR 0.01, top 12 by count, most genes covered).
  Pool 2 sources x 9 variants x 3 statistics = 54 candidates.
- DEVIATION (amendment_1, before any list or statistic): the brief's floor 0.32 is a PAIR quantile; the calibration
  showed it does not bound a per-genome MAXIMUM, and the label-free chance model I predeclared (mirrored lower tail)
  is invalid because the zca10 cosine distribution is strongly asymmetric. Floors became a pool dimension of global
  cosines tuned on odd genomes (section 1).
