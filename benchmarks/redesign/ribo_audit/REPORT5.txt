1. DEFINITIONS (predeclared5.json)
  Common to every definition below: the part-4 member lists (union for every genome), the chain with <= 2 other genes between
  consecutive members, the package g2_m16 runs, and the recovered / resolved / any-fit rules. Chance uses 5 same-contig windows
  per target of the target length, seed 17.
  - standing: the package run.
  - part 4: the strand-blind chain containing the run.
  - (a) member-strand: break between consecutive members on opposite strands.
  - (b) gap-strand: (a) plus a break at any gene between two consecutive members that lies on the opposite strand.
  - (c) connector: (a), except an opposite-strand member junction is kept when its connector is <= 1/3 over >= 5 other lineage
    groups.
    - Connector = mean over the other groups of the same domain of the share of that group's instances of the family pair
      lying on the same strand. It is the cross-lineage form of ELSA d297985's same_frac.
    - Reason stated in advance: a conserved relative orientation marks a conserved neighbourhood.
  Strand-aware targets:
  - Pieces = chains holding marked genes of the run. A piece qualifies with >= 16 package-marked genes.
  - A run is resolved when all its qualifying pieces are resolved.
  - A run with no qualifying piece is BELOW MINIMUM: it leaves the benchmark, and it counts as unresolved in "res/orig".
  - No run produced two qualifying pieces except one SR-VP bacterial run under (b).
  Code check: bio5.py reproduces the part-4 cluster-level and standing numbers and chance in every cell, on all three datasets.
