HEAD WORKSTREAM: REPORT (2026-11-12, DPANN package, ESM++ small; learned head on stored vectors)
==================================================================================================

QUESTION
Can one learned vector per gene (a small network on the stored ESM++ small zca10 vectors, trained so that
genes with an MMseqs2 alignment are close) replace MMseqs2 ranks in locus search and MMseqs2 families in the
recurrence engine?

ANSWER IN SEVEN LINES
1. In locus search one vector per gene replaces MMseqs2 ranks, and most of the work is done by the
   unsupervised zca10 transform. Even-genome DefenseFinder (1,247 queries): zca10 ranks alone (M1z) AP 0.792,
   student ranks alone (M1') 0.809, against MMseqs2 ranks alone (S1) 0.602 and the three-input S3 0.698.
   Round 1's M1 = 0.442 used uncentred L2 vectors (homolog retrieval AP@2000 0.336 against 0.722 for zca10).
2. H1 as declared: NOT MET on the rate condition. AP passes (0.809 >= 0.600); unsupported unlabelled hits are
   0.065 [0.042, 0.094] per query against a bound of 0.05. Under the same declared 200-replicate null, round-1 M1
   sits at 0.137 and S3 at 0.029; the bound was copied from round 2's calibrated-null table, where M1 was 0.042.
   Tokens + student (M2', two inputs) passes both: AP 0.821, 0.020 unsupported per query.
3. The learned head's own contribution is small: M1' - M1z +0.017 [+0.013, +0.021] AP, and it halves the
   unsupported rate (0.121 -> 0.065). H3 NOT MET: retrieval AP@2000 +0.075 [+0.071, +0.079] over zca10 (bar 0.10).
4. S3' exceeds S3 by +0.128 [+0.121, +0.135] (the student adds to sequence ranks more than the L2 embedding
   did); +0.118 of that comes from zca10 alone (S3z - S3) and +0.010 [+0.007, +0.013] from the head.
5. H2 NOT MET. The chosen admissible student-family arm (r3000, 27,405 tokens) is -0.070 [-0.087, -0.054]
   below the MMseqs2 base in summed DefenseFinder + TXSScan excess; the only arm above the base (r19000,
   +0.021 [+0.001, +0.042]) loses the ribosomal locus (1,144 of 1,686 runs, 35 of 114 Parvarchaeaceae).
   Student families are no better than raw zca10 families at matched token counts.
6. Design ingredients (validation genomes): hard negatives +0.006, family-co-member positives -0.005, bit-score
   weighting +0.001 (n.s.); a linear 960 -> 256 map beats every MLP (MLPs overfit after 1,500 of 12,000 steps).
7. Practical reading: for search, apply zca10 (centring + soft whitening) to the stored vectors; a linear head
   adds +0.017 AP. For discovery, MMseqs2 families (rescue3) stay the default.

READINGS AND WHAT WOULD HAVE REFUTED THEM (written in NOTEBOOK.txt before the test numbers)
  (1) "The round-1 embedding deficit in search was list length (a rank list gives all 20,000 entries
      s >= 5.2)": refuted. zca10 lists of the same length reach 0.792; the deficit was the uncentred L2 space.
  (2) "The student's information is already in MMseqs2 ranks": refuted for search; S3' - S3 = +0.128, and the
      head alone adds +0.010 on top of zca10 + sequence.
  (3) "A head taught family co-membership keeps universal families whole at matched token counts": not
      supported; ribosomal recovery tracks token count as for V0 (student better at ~110k, worse at ~65k).
  (4) "zca10 ranks buy primary AP with spurious windows": not supported; strict AP rises with primary AP
      (0.257 -> 0.510), 95% of M1z unlabelled hits carry the query type's DefenseFinder profile domains, and
      the unsupported rate (0.121) is below round-1 M1's (0.137) under the same null.

CAVEATS
  - The H1 rate bound (0.05) came from round 2's calibrated-null table (M1 0.042); under the declared
    200-replicate null round-1 M1 is 0.137. I report the declared verdict; no calibrated E-values were computed
    for these runs (their null tails were not stored).
  - zca10's mean and covariance are computed over all 2.5 M sequences, including even genomes (unsupervised,
    transductive; no labels enter).
  - Retrieval queries come from the S search (356 random genomes plus gold-locus neighbourhoods); 97% have a
    saturated prefilter, handled by amendment 1.
  - Validation retrieval was the only selection signal for the head; a locus-search signal on validation genomes
    was not used.
  - Wrong-type hits per query at E <= 0.01 for M1' (1.35) match the sequence methods (S3 1.22), above M0/M2.
