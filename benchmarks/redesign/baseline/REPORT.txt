CAVEATS
- One embedding model: ESM++ small (the ESM-C 300M architecture), the only vectors on DPANN; no other
  model was run on DPANN. On Spacedust ESM2-650M and ESM-C 6B legacy runs scored best among ESM2 / ESM-C.
- Legacy = research checkout as shipped: no neighbourhood selection (that step is production-only), no
  anchor extension, clustering jaccard_tau 0.9. Blocks are scored, clusters are reported only.
- The union reading is one natural single reading of overlapping blocks; others exist (cluster
  footprints, a block-support ladder). The legacy pipeline ships blocks, not loci, so "single reading"
  is constructed here; the all-interval reading is the shipped output itself.
- Calibration uses the Spacedust rule unchanged; on DPANN it acts as a size filter (section 2), so the
  calibrated rows measure "blocks of >= 4-5 anchors", which miss 2-gene systems by construction.
- The engine full table is best-of-11 and legacy all-interval tables are best-of-hundreds; their excesses
  carry their own random controls but the two oracles differ in strength. Single vs single is the
  comparison to quote.
- Engine runtime is not comparable here: it needs the MMseqs2 families (precomputed; the engine itself
  runs in about 96 s once tokens exist).
