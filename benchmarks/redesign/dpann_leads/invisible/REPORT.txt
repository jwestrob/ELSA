BOTTOM LINE
1. YES. Profile-invisible, unannotated enzyme calls pass a structure-and-residue assay at 25/132 = 18.9% (Wilson 95%
   13.2-26.5%) in the 16 families whose positive controls work; the negatives pass at 0/354 (0-1.1%), so the expected
   false passes among 132 genes are at most 1.4. One-sided Fisher p = 1.2e-15. Positive controls pass 35/38 (92.1%).
2. The yield is concentrated: 15 of the 25 passes are one family (GalP_UDP_transf label, function K00965). Without it,
   10/117 (8.5%) still beats the negatives (p = 6.6e-7). Passes sit in 6 of 16 informative families.
3. The function source decides the outcome: partner-derived functions pass 22/33 (66.7%); the label-level fallback passes
   3/99 (3.0%). By list type, embedding-only 15/49 (30.6%) vs both lists 9/70 (12.9%); every embedding-only pass is a
   GalT-like gene (0/34 embedding-only passes outside that family).
4. Best lead, SUPPORTED: a GalT-branch HIT nucleotidyltransferase in Woesearchaeales and QMZS01 glycogen loci, 15/15
   folded unannotated genes pass (H186 equivalent, UDP-glucose residues, TM 0.63-0.64), function absent otherwise in
   15/15 genomes, all embedding-only, 14/15 partner-derived; a homodimer co-fold puts UDP-glucose phosphorus 3.2-3.3 A
   from the His exactly as in the positive control. Name: consistent with UDP-glucose:hexose-1-P uridylyltransferase;
   substrate and pathway role UNVERIFIED (galactokinase is absent from 704/704 Woesearchaeales genomes).
5. Second, SUPPORTED: two Amidase_3 Zn amidases (Woesearchaeales) with a complete Zn site; co-fold coordinates Zn like
   the positive. Also passing: 4 ThyA and 2 PriS (housekeeping), 1 PRPP synthase and 1 M32 (genome already has one).
6. One family keeps the fold and lacks the catalytic residue: PEPCK_GTP, 11/11 fold, catalytic Cys 0/11 (separate table).

ASSAY (assay_rule.json, written before any fold; amendment recorded there)
  ESMFold2 fast (Biohub) single chain; US-align on the biotite login node against the reference chain trimmed to the
  label-Pfam region. PASS = TM normalized by the reference >= 0.50, every UniProt Active-site residue identical at the
  structurally aligned position within 5 A (US-align ':'), >= 50% of Binding-site residues the same way, mean pLDDT of
  the model residues at the catalytic positions >= 70. Accepted substitutions: none documented, identity required.
  Controls: positive = DPANN proteins with the KO (KOfam) and the family Pfam at GA (2-4 per family); negative A = the same
  candidates against the reference of a different family of closest length; negative B = length-matched (+-15%) DPANN
  proteins with no Pfam row and without the label (140 reused check-B folds of the identical model, 18 new folds).
  Pilot gate (6 families, 55 folds): positives 15/19, negative A 0/36, negative B 0/36, Fisher p = 3.3e-10: passed.
  Amendment (after the full-run folds were requested, before scoring): the first novelty pass missed CDD models that
  name the function in words (COG1085 'Galactose-1-phosphate uridylyltransferase' on 47/64 GalT-labeled genes); the
  added COG/name rule moved 45 folded candidates into a 'named' stratum (25 GalT, 11 PEPCK, 4 Amidase_3, 2 RelA, 2 ThyA,
  1 MTAP); each of the 45 placements was inspected and names the function. Named stratum passes 28/45 (62.2%).
