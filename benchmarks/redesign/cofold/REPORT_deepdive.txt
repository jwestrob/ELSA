ws_cofold3 - deep dive on the two remaining showcase candidates (DPANN, engine v1.5 results_dpann_v5)
12 November 2026. Biohub ESMFold2 (esmfold2-2026-05, fold_all_atom). Names below are observations of
similarity unless a curated caller emitted the call; curated resources in the live data: DefenseFinder
(defense_systems), TXSScan (secretion_systems: Archaeal-T4P 804, T5aSS 4, T5bSS 1), CRISPRCasTyper.

HEADLINE
- Candidate 1 is the first gene pair of a three-gene operon A (60 aa) - B (70 aa) - C (214 aa). A and B stay
  unrecognised by every profile and structure search; C is recognised. C sits immediately downstream of B in 258 of
  267 instances (co-oriented in all 258, overlapping in 168), its family resembles CD-clan (caspase-like / CHAT)
  cysteine proteases by HHsearch and Foldseek, and DefenseFinder emitted a curated "Borvo" call (component BovA)
  on C in 8 of 268 loci. The folds are robust: AB co-folds in 12 instances over 7 phyla (ipTM 0.65-0.94,
  median 0.78; partner swaps median 0.13, max 0.47), as a 1:1 heterodimer, and ABC co-folds as a trimer (ipTM
  median 0.905 in 11/12; C' swap controls median 0.29). B binds C directly over C's conserved His-Cys pair.
  Fused A-B single-ORF homologs exist in DPANN (3) and bacteria (2 UniProt entries) and fold into the AB
  arrangement (TM 0.68-0.82). Best reading: two unrecognised small proteins forming a conserved heterodimer that
  packs onto a caspase-like protein of a defense-associated operon; a "two-protein dark heterodimer" framing is
  refuted as incomplete (the unit has a recognised third partner).
- Candidate 2 (DUF7287/DUF7288 cassette) fails as a showcase: it is recognised (Pfam DUF7287/7288 by HHsearch,
  TXSScan arCOG06117 component on members of its rank-7 chain-A family, Foldseek minor-pilin EpdB), it sits 1-2
  genes from curated Archaeal-T4P call members whenever the contig carries a call (8/8), and its screen-2
  interfaces do not hold as isolated pairs (1/18 pass full length) and survive truncation of the leader + alpha1-N
  helix only in some replicates. Pilin-like controls stay low (72 full-length control pairs: median ipTM 0.18,
  0 >= 0.75), so a generic pilin-helix artefact is not what drives the screen-2 signal; the cassette is not
  identical to any earlier lead (0 shared genes with cluster:1043 or cluster:3913 loci).

 - Split gene / frameshift artefact: tested. Fusions in 3/295 genomes (1 %); B starts with its own ATG in an ATGA
   overlap in 64 % of pairs; fused proteins fold into the AB arrangement. Not refuted (nucleotide frameshift-motif
   scan of the overlaps not run for this pair).
 - Sticky small proteins: tested. 36 swap controls max 0.47 vs cognate min 0.65. Not refuted.
 - Disorder zipping: tested, 0/12. Not refuted.
 - Higher-order / wrong stoichiometry: tested; 1:1 heterodimer. Not refuted.
 - Recognised by profile or structure: tested for A and B (HHblits/HHsearch, UniRef50 HMM, Foldseek): not refuted.
 - "Two proteins" / no known partner: REFUTED. A and B form a trimer with C (11/12, controls 0.29), C is

   defence-gene neighbourhood (3.5x curated DefenseFinder members).
 - Deep conservation: holds (249 genomes, 21 orders, 7 phyla; CPR bacteria homologs) but presence is patchy
   (4-21 % per phylum), never near-universal.
 - No coevolution evidence: MSA folds equal single-sequence folds (median ipTM 0.762 vs 0.784); interface columns
   not more conserved than others (p 0.81).
