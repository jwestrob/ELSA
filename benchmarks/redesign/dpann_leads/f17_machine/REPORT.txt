F17 MACHINE: WHAT THE MICRARCHAEOTA FAMILY-17 LOCUS ENCODES (2026-11-21 UTC)
=============================================================================
BOTTOM LINE
Family 17 is two units. A pan-DPANN housekeeping pair (topoisomerase VI subunit B + KRR1-type KH protein) sits beside a
separate co-directional operon that encodes the core of a Ced-type, T4SS-derived membrane apparatus: a VirB4/CedB-family
ATPase split into two genes (the C-terminal motor, ATPase504, and its N-terminal domain as two paralogous 2-TM proteins,
TM277a/TM278b), 1-6 paralogs of a CedA-type multi-pass channel protein (MP4610), and a VirB2-family pilin (pil129), plus
lineage-specific accessories with no Ced counterpart (p102 ATPase partner, a Zn-ribbon 2-TM protein, a CdvB-like
winged-helix protein, a basic 49-aa peptide, an N-anchored surface protein, a GT-C glycosyltransferase).
- Every component call rests on profile-profile alignment against the characterized Sulfolobus/Aeropyrum Ced proteins
  (CedB prob 95.5-100, CedA prob 94-96, VirB2 prob 96.4; 204/224 unrelated role-reference pairs score < 50, median 0.1),
  confirmed by structure (AFDB-SwissProt top hit CedB for both halves of the split motor) and by a fusion test.
- The "A2M-like" protein fails as an alpha-2-macroglobulin: thioester motif C.EQ in 0/555, no A2M profile hit, structure
  similarity limited to Ig-like beta-sandwich domains (qTM 0.10-0.13). It is an N-anchored surface protein of unknown role.
- Restricted to Micrarchaeota (708/1,146 genomes carry the motor; 0 ortholog-level hits in 2,418 other DPANN genomes, one
  7-gene contig exception). The 125 Nanobdellota / 7 Aenigmatarchaeota "Top6B + DUF87" cases are unrelated DUF87 ATPases.
- NAMED status: no curated secretion-system caller in the database emitted a call for this locus; the identity below is
  "consistent with a Ced-type DNA-import apparatus", UNVERIFIED. Function (DNA import versus another cargo) is the open
  question; composition and architecture are well supported.

Q1. ONE UNIT OR TWO -> TWO
- Strand: in 582 ATPase-oriented loci the run pil129-p244b-MP4610(n)-TM278b-TM277a-ATPase504-p102-TM132-wHTH244-p49-
  A2Ml794 is co-directional (every role >= 99% same strand); Top6B (477/477) and KH171 (462/462) sit on the opposite strand,
  convergent with A2Ml794 (median gap 9 bp); ArsR178 is divergent from KH171 (median 241 bp).
- Spacing inside the machine is operon-like: ATPase504-p102 median 2 bp (p10 -7, overlapping), TM277a-TM278b 0 bp,
  TM132-p102 6 bp, p49-wHTH244 5 bp, ATPase504-TM277a 32 bp.
- The method's own bonds (Z bonds, d = 1): Top6B-KH171 lambda median 959 (n = 2,010 genomes, 399 groups); A2Ml794-Top6B is
  the weakest junction (bonded in 186/464 adjacent pairs, lambda 8.7, n = 3); inside the motor ATPase504-TM277a lambda 98.5
  (565/565 bonded, n = 542), TM132-wHTH244 35.3, ATPase504-p102 31.5. Tree nodes: the commonest nested nodes are
  {ATPase504, TM132, TM277a, TM278b, p102, wHTH244} (523), {KH171, Top6B} (473), {MP4610, p244b, pil129} (350).
- Top6B-KH adjacency is pan-DPANN and independent of the machine: KH within 2 genes of Top6B in Nanobdellota 803/1,162,
  Aenigmatarchaeota 207/303, Iainarchaeota 133/182, Nanohalarchaeota 41/46; the canonical DPANN neighborhood is
  KH-Top6B-Top6A (Top6A within 2 genes in 1,083/1,162 Nanobdellota). In Micrarchaeales Top6A is never within 2 genes of Top6B
  (0/748); the machine occupies that side.
- Co-occurrence (795 Micrarchaeota genomes, completeness >= 80): motor with Top6B-KH adjacency 402; motor without it 102;
  adjacency without motor 125. Outside Micrarchaeales the motor never sits beside Top6B (Anstonellales 38/38,
  Fermentimicrarchaeales 7/7, JAKAKT01 4/4 motor loci have no Top6B in the +-6 window; e.g. locus 26611 = TM277a-ATPase504-
  p102-TM132-wHTH244-A2Ml794 alone). A2Ml794 travels with the motor (457 together; 35 + 47 discordant).
- Topo VI is ordinary. Top6B (400 members vs S. shibatae O05207): GHKL N42, D76, K98, G109, G111 >= 99%, transducer K427
  100%; model TM 0.74-0.85 to Top6B crystal structures. Top6A elsewhere (502/599 genomes; O05208): Y106, E209, D261, D263 100%.

Q3. WHICH KNOWN MACHINE (machines.tsv)
- Favored: Ced (Sulfolobales DNA import; CedA/CedA1/CedA2/CedB, PMID 26884154, 36750723). Present: CedB (split), CedA
  (paralogs). Replaced: CedA1-family pilin -> VirB2-family pilin. Absent: CedA2 (p49 has 0 TM). Extra: six accessories.
- Ted (Thermoproteales) is less likely: channel aligns to CedA (94-96) and not TedA (2-10); pilin aligns to VirB2, not TedC.
- Monoderm conjugative T4SS (MPF_FATA; Patescibacteria T4SS, PMID 40408144) shares the split VirB4 (TrsD/TrsE) but its
  VirB8 (0/611 loci), VirB1/SLT (0/611), VirD4 (TrwB_AAD_bind 2/611, TraG-D_C 0/611) and relaxase (0/611) are absent and the
  channel is CedA-type, not VirB6/PrgH-type. Diderm T4SS, archaeal T4P/archaella, HerA-NurA (NurA/Rad50/Mre11 0/611),
  FtsK/T7SS (FHA, WXG100 0/611) and Cdv division (one domain-level match) are disfavored. Margin: Ced 4/5 vs next 2/5.
- A2M partner check: bacterial A2M works with PBP1C; transglycosylase is absent from Micrarchaeota (Transgly 0 genomes,
  Transpeptidase 1/1,146). True A2M-Pfam proteins exist in 16 Micrarchaeota genomes and are unrelated to F17.

Q7. ORIGIN
- ATPase tree (37 sequences, 222 informative sites, LG+F+G4, 1,000 UFBoot; tree/): F17 ATPases sit inside the VirB4
  superclade (UFBoot 88) with Sulfolobus CedB and a second Micrarchaeota VirB4-family protein (76), then A. pernix CedB, TedB
  and TcpF (37). Bacterial VirB4s (diderm, monoderm, Saccharibacteria) and archaeal plasmid TraG-like, HerA and the DPANN
  Top6B-near DUF87 proteins fall outside. Archaeal Ced branch, moderate support.
- A2Ml794: no tree. No sequence-alignable relative outside Micrarchaeota (0 any-score hits; UniRef30 adds no family);
  structure matches are single Ig-like domains. Origin unresolved.

IDENTITY
Most likely: a Ced-type DNA-import apparatus (domesticated T4SS) with a split CedB motor, CedA-type channel paralogs and a
VirB2-family pilin, extended in Micrarchaeota by accessories including a surface Ig-like protein.
- For: profile-profile homology to three Ced components with near-zero nulls; split exactly at the CedB domain boundary
  (TM277a : CedB 106-222, ATPase504 : CedB 255-579) with the fusion test; CedB as top structural neighbor; tree beside CedB;
  operon packing; chromosomal, conserved position in 599 genomes with no relaxase or VirD4, as in Ced.
- Against: cargo and direction are untested by any of this; Ced function is characterized only in Sulfolobales; six genes
  have no Ced counterpart; CedA2 is missing; co-folds give no motor oligomer or motor-membrane interface.
Alternative 1: a T4SS-derived contact apparatus that exports protein or DNA into the Thermoplasmatales host (episymbiont
  lifestyle; cf. Patescibacteria T4SS essential for epibiotic growth). Alternative 2: a conjugation-like apparatus for DNA
  exchange between Micrarchaeota cells (Ted-like).
Falsifier: carrier cells that express the operon (pil129 / ATPase504 detected) yet take up no labeled DNA, or operon
  expression that tracks host attachment and ignores DNA-damage stress, would retire the import identity.
Decisive experiment: in an enrichment with a carrier (e.g. a Micrarchaeales co-culture), add labeled DNA (EdU-tagged or
  fluorescent) after UV, and image uptake with immunolocalized pil129 / ATPase504; the cheapest computational test is
  metatranscriptomic co-expression of the operon with DNA-repair genes after stress in existing Micrarchaeota datasets.
