C and D keep every locus; G and H lose coverage on TXSScan and CRISPR-Cas beyond the 0.01 bar (H CCT -0.031),
so for them "without losing loci" holds only for the ribosomal locus (recovered 1,680-1,685). More, shorter loci
also raise the random-placement F1 (control change C +.027/+.058/+.035, G +.049/+.084/+.057, H +.022/+.051/+.029),
so excess gains are smaller than F1 gains; D's TXSScan gain is mostly localization (+.226 F1, control +.043).

Mechanism. Gold system edges coincide with changes of the module of the pair that sets the boundary's support
(AUROC internal vs overshoot boundaries, odd: DF .833, TXSS .753, CCT .740), while support level alone separates
them weakly and in the wrong direction for CRISPR-Cas (log b AUROC .377: CRISPR-Cas members sit at lower support
than their level-2 neighbours) and strongly for the ribosomal run (.946). At Louvain res 0.3, 1,547 of 1,686
ribosomal runs carry one module label on every internal boundary; at res 3 and 10 (the engine's own), 1,566-1,575
carry two, which is why the unit/module readings cut the run with the engine's modules. D works differently: its
level-200 core exists only for pairs present in >= 200 genera (the ribosomal run and many TXSScan-called loci); every
other instance is read on the level-2 locus, which is why DF and CCT are unchanged and Parvarchaeaceae resolve
13/114 (their ribosomal pairs reach fewer genera).

conditions in 5 of 5 seeds (H summed +.100 to +.117, ribosomal 1,494-1,554, Parv resolved 114/114 every seed).
Resolution 0.2 / 0.25 / 0.35 / 0.5: H and G pass at all four; C fails (ii) at 0.2 and 0.5.

DECISION FOR THE COORDINATOR (one line each)
- Default reading: D (adopted by the predeclared rule; keeps every level-2 locus, adds universal cores) or H (one
  reading; best ribosomal and F1 on all callers; drops 9.9% of genes and some CRISPR-Cas coverage; depends on a
  Louvain resolution of 0.3 that differs from the engine's 10). D + H core (level-2 neighbourhood + H core) is the
  untested combination that would keep coverage and add H's localization.
- The DPANN headline numbers should state which reading they score; the full table's F1 is a best-of-11 figure.
