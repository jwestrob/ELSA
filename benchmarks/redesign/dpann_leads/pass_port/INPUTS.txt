store n and ng, the frozen bonds store Lambda only, so the bond gate recomputes Lambda from the lists with vl.pass_count's
group-counting formula (exact; testset3 smoke: 17,260 / 17,260 bonds reproduced); DPANN list tau 6, frozen tau 8 (each
collection's own rule); label tables and the KO -> Pfam proxy table are computed on each collection's own annotation.
