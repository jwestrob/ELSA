RIBOSOMAL-LOCUS CHECK FOR THE RECURRENCE ENGINE (DPANN) - workstream ws_ribosomal, 2026-11-12

VERDICT
The redesigned engine finds the ribosomal-protein locus in every genome that carries one, as a
single locus, and its default reading overshoots the locus ends.
 - Base arm (MMseqs2 e3c50s75 families, w3), default reading conserved@2: 1,686/1,686 runs recovered,
   1,640 inside exactly one locus, median coverage 1.000. Resolved (one locus >= 80% of the run at
   precision >= 0.8): 711/1,686 (42.2%). Of the 975 unresolved runs, 958 fail on precision alone:
   the locus continues a median 3 genes past each run end into supported conserved neighbours.
   Predeclared verdict: PARTIALLY RESOLVED (recovered 100% >= 90%, control gap +48.4 points >= 30,
   resolved 42.2% falls between the 30% refutation line and the 50% support line).
 - The same support landscape read at level 80 (boundaries spanned by pairs in >= 80 genera) resolves
   1,317/1,686 (78.1%) at median precision 0.923, with 1,677 recovered.
 - Best setting: genome-centred ESM++ small merge of the e3c50 families (hybrid ...e3c50cpmr0p05),
   read as profile units (unit@0.5): 1,565/1,686 resolved (92.8%), 1,682 recovered, median precision
   0.96, random windows 0.01% resolved. Against the base arm's best fixed reading (conserved@80):
   +248 runs [218, 278] (paired genome bootstrap, 2,000 draws). All ten hybrid arms resolve
   1,471-1,565 by the unit reading. This meets every predeclared support criterion.
 - Larger windows (w5, w8, adaptive) and over-split token arms (embedding-only graph families at high
   resolution; context "all"/"permtop" families) resolve fewer runs; the most over-split arms lose
   the locus entirely (6-928 of 1,686 recovered).

3. DIAGNOSIS (base arm; statistics recomputed with the engine's own functions: 159,955 significant
   pairs and boundary_support.npy reproduced exactly)
What would refute each reading is stated with it.
a) Fragmentation is not the failure. 1,640/1,686 runs lie inside one conserved@2 locus (40 in 2,
   6 in 3). 87 of 39,659 boundaries inside runs carry no significant pair (52 runs). Break rate:
   0.08% between two marked genes, 0.65% where a flank gene is unmarked. Features at breaks against
   non-breaks (predeclared bar: >= 2x): unmarked flank gene 71% vs 24% (3.0x), flank gene that is an
   endpoint of no significant pair 60% vs 4.8% (12x), flank token present in <= 1 genome 13.8% vs
   2.6% (5.4x; 12 breaks). Best spanning pair at breaks has L >= 2 in 79/87 (the P <= 1e-4 cut
   fails), L = 1 in 8. Families bordering breaks: Ribosomal_L3 22 of 1,590 flank boundaries,
   Ribosomal_S10 13/27, Ribosomal_S13 8/203, Ribosomal_S8 8/3,347. Run genes that are endpoints of a
   significant pair: 99.9% of marked, 82.7% of unmarked (genome-wide 63%).
b) The failure is overshoot at the run ends. 958/975 unresolved runs have one locus covering >= 80%
   of the run. Genes added past the run ends at conserved@2: median 8 per run (q90 36.5, max 245;
   24,668 genes in 1,542 runs). Observed Pfam domains on the gene adjacent to the run end: SecY
   427/1,192 (left) and 419/1,166 (right), Plug_translocon, PK/PK_C, DNA_primase_lrg/_S, KOW, TrmB,
   CBFD_NFYB_HMF, TAF, Histone; about 36% carry no Pfam hit. The most frequent adjacent neighbour is
   token 68605 (3,316 of its 3,318 genes carry SecY; present in 3,280 genomes), joined to the run by
   pairs in up to 667 genera. The level-2 locus is therefore the conserved neighbourhood around the
   Pfam-marked run; the gold run boundary (last marked gene) is narrower than it, and "resolved"
   measures agreement with the marked run. Raising the level trims the overshoot (ext 3/3 -> 0/0,
   precision 0.743 -> 0.923) at a cost of 9 recovered runs. Refutation for "supported neighbourhood,
   not noise": random windows would match the observed precision; they resolve 6.7% (@2) and 0.3%
   (@80) against 42.2% and 78.1%.
c) Window size: w5, w8 and adaptive a8 bridge further past the run ends (precision 0.57-0.65,
   resolved@2 -184 to -258, every interval excludes 0); adaptive a3 gives -27 [-56, 1] and recovers
   only 1,605. w3 is the best window for conserved readings. The unit reading responds to the window
   unevenly: e3c50s75 w3 149, w5 1,355; id30c80 w3 0, w5 721, w8 13.
d) Family granularity: base tokens keep ribosomal families nearly whole (median 2 tokens per Pfam
   family, top-token share 0.995, tokens in a median 3,097 genomes), so granularity does not limit
   the base. It limits split arms: id30c80 (5.5 tokens per family, share 0.69) loses 388 at @80;
   graph arms (13.5-26 tokens, share 0.18-0.47) recover 1,646-1,681 but lose the high levels;
   ctx "all" arms (37-150 tokens per family) and "permtop" arms (130-164, share 0.02-0.04) lose the
   locus (recovered 6-1,580; Parvarchaeaceae 0-53/114). Hybrid arms merge each family to one token
   (share 1.0) and are the ones whose unit reading resolves the run. Table: results/diag/granularity.tsv.
e) Locus-reading rule: in the base arm unit@0.5 cuts runs into a median 3 units (4,660 internal cuts in
   1,665 runs; most frequent cuts after Ribosomal_S17 before an unmarked gene 681, Ribosomal_S17 |
   Ribosomal_L14 589, Ribosomal_L3 | Ribosomal_L4 435). In hybrid r0p05 the cuts fall to 1,796 in 817
   runs and the best unit covers the run in 1,565. The reading rule (level, unit) moves resolved counts
   from 149 to 1,317 on one arm; the token arm moves the unit reading from 149 to 1,565.
f) Modules: the run's marked genes fall in two Louvain modules in every genome: 329 (57.3% of marked
   genes; Pfam names Ribosomal_L3, L4, L23, L2_C, S19, L22, S3_C, L29, S17, L14, L26, S4e, L5, L5_C)
   and 85 (40.4%; Ribosomal_S14, S8, L6, L5e, L32e, L19e, S5, S5_C, L30, L27A, L34e); module 155
   (Ribosomal_L13, S9, S11, S13, S4, L18) holds 451 genes. Median 2 gene modules per run. Top-module
   purity of the best locus over recovered runs is 0.849 (329 in 1,432, 85 in 251; random windows
   0.149), which meets the predeclared 0.8 bar; the per-gene view shows a consistent two-module
   split of one locus at Louvain resolution 10.

OPEN
- The precision criterion counts the SecY-domain and other supported neighbours as error. A gold
  definition that includes neighbours present in most genomes would change the resolved counts;
  none was tried here.
- The unit reading wins only with merged (hybrid) tokens; whether a unit reading with Jaccard tau
  other than 0.5 or a wider window fixes the MMseqs base without the merge is untested (the engine
  wrote only tau 0.5 to the all-genome table).
