ANSWER IN TEN LINES
1. The genus can be replaced by data-derived GROUPS, but only at a scale coarser than the genus, and the threshold
   that reaches that scale comes from odd-genome calibration. The internal criterion picks the species gap and
   fails. Weights (L_eff) fail at every threshold.
2. Internal criterion (predeclared): the antimode of the genomes' nearest-neighbour similarity. Embedding mean
   best-match cosine: 0.753 (1.50x deep), giving 1,965 groups. Homolog-level cosine: 0.894 (2.67x), giving 1,882
   groups. These are GTDB species (AMI .904 / .963). Family Jaccard has no antimode (deepest 1.21x). The engine has
   no plateau to read either: significant pairs rise monotonically with the number of units (89k at 172 groups,
   163k at 922 genera, 288k at 1,965, 548k at 3,564 genomes).
3. Species-gap units DO NOT replace the genus (token engine, even DH): summed +0.010/+0.011 (groups) and
   +0.025/+0.028 (weights), but CRISPR-Cas F1 -0.025 to -0.043 and ribosomal resolved 1,407-1,412 (-100 to -105,
   -6.6 to -6.9%). emb_mean groups also lose DefenseFinder F1 (-0.029).
4. Odd-selected coarse groups REPLACE the genus on the token engine (even DH). Each meets every condition:
     fam_jac 0.2 (232 groups): +0.017 [+0.002,+0.033]; F1 +.005/-.003/+.005; resolved 1,487 (-1.7%); Parv 114 / 107

   ribosomal runs against 704. Post hoc, fam_jac 0.25 and emb_mean 0.525 also pass. The band is about 170-650 groups
   for every similarity, and the neighbouring points each miss one condition by a small margin.
5. Token-free round 2 (own-group exclusion in the lists, group counts, Leiden r2000, Louvain 0.3, DH). Only emb_hom
   0.75 replaces the genus: +0.019 [+0.006,+0.032]; F1 -.000/-.000/-.008; resolved 1,271 against 1,263; Parv 113.
   emb_mean 0.55 fails on CRISPR-Cas F1 (-0.024 [-0.031,-0.017]) at +0.029 summed. fam_jac 0.2 fails on CRISPR-Cas
   F1 (-0.044) and recovers 108 of 114 Parvarchaeaceae runs, at +0.078 summed. The species-gap units fail:
   -0.072 and -0.066. Every genome as its own lineage gives -0.154.
6. What the unit buys (every genome its own lineage). Token engine: DH -0.020 [-0.041,-0.000], F1 -.094/-.019/-.098,
   resolved -148; R1 -0.124. Token-free: DH -0.154, R1 -0.221.
7. Mechanism: the engine's null shuffles gene order inside each genome independently, so it cannot see order
   inherited within a clade. Every finer unit turns shared ancestry into recurrence. Significant pairs grow with
   the unit count, loci extend (DH genes in loci 2.56M under genus, 2.78M under species units), and DefenseFinder
   and CRISPR-Cas F1 fall. TXSScan F1 rises (+0.018 to +0.027). What the genus supplies is a coarse partition of
   related genomes. The data match it at family-like scale: the passing groups have AMI .78-.82 with GTDB family.
   Relatedness matters, not only the number of units. Random partitions with genus group sizes (post hoc) behave
   like "every genome its own lineage" (548k significant pairs, DF/CCT F1 -0.09). The gap criterion finds species
   because similarity has a gap there; genera are rank-normalised, with no gap.
8. Recommendation: groups by average linkage on 1 - S, not weights. For one unit serving both routes: the
   homolog-level best-match cosine (emb_hom) at theta 0.75. For the token engine alone, needing nothing beyond its
   own family table: family Jaccard at theta 0.2 (15 s similarity + 20 s grouping). All thresholds are calibrated on
   DPANN odd genomes. The passing band is narrow in theta and consistent in scale (about one group per 6-20
   genomes; Section 4).
9. Reproduction: the genus map fed through both copies reproduces the deployed outputs exactly. Token engine:
   significant pairs, rules, boundary support, modules, all-genome loci, R1/DH loci and per-instance scores.
   Token-free: L, G, E and W, sidx, b, edge endpoints, Leiden families and per-instance R1/DH scores. Edge cosines
   differ by at most 3e-7 (float32), and the partition is unchanged.
10. Running the pipeline needs taxonomy in three places, each replaced in this workstream's copies by a lineage map:
   the engine Universe (genus -> L), the softrec Universe (genus -> list exclusion and L), and the engine pair
   statistics behind the token-free Louvain weights. Every other read is reporting or evaluation (Section 5).

CAVEATS
- No internal criterion reached the working scale: thresholds that pass are calibrated on DPANN odd genomes (gold),
  in similarity units that depend on the family clustering (fam_jac) or the embedding model and whitening
  (emb_mean, emb_hom). A new dataset or model needs the same calibration, or a scale rule (Section 4b).
- The odd selection score maximises odd summed excess among units meeting the most conditions, so it picks
  coarse units; the fam_jac pick (0.2) sat at the edge of its predeclared ladder (extended post hoc).
- emb_hom 0.75 passes the token engine by margins under 0.001 (summed -0.0093, TXSS F1 -0.0096).
- The DH reading's Louvain resolution (0.3) and trimming (0.5) were selected on the genus landscape and were not
  re-tuned for any unit; ribosomal resolution is the condition this most affects.
- Token-free edge cosines are not bit-reproducible (<= 3e-7); the Leiden partition was unaffected in the genus
  rerun.
- Two diagnostics on the login node ran 67 s and ~20 s (over the 30 s guidance once: an edge-array comparison).

NEXT (not started; one line each)
- A scale rule in place of a theta: cut the dendrogram at a fixed group count relative to the genome count (about
  1 per 6-20 genomes), or a fixed number of merges above the species gap. Test it on a second dataset, because the
  DPANN calibration fixes only one point.
- Re-select the DH reading's Louvain resolution and trimming on the adopted unit (odd genomes). The remaining
  ribosomal deficit (-1.7% to -2.4%) comes from parameters tuned on the genus landscape.
