HIERARCHY WORKSTREAM: REPORT (2026-11-12, DPANN package; ESM++ small V0 Leiden levels + MMseqs2 levels)
==========================================================================================================

QUESTION
Does one hierarchy of families (nested or multi-resolution) from one input serve discovery (recurrence engine)
and search, with evidence for two genes read as -log(size of the smallest shared cluster / N)?

ANSWER IN SEVEN LINES
1. T1 CONFIRMED (3 of 3 new seeds), AND EVERY SEED IS INADMISSIBLE. V0 r19000 beats the base on even genomes by
   +0.039 / +0.038 / +0.038 (seeds 18-20; seed 17 +0.038), and recovers 1,122-1,165 of 1,686 ribosomal runs
   (Parvarchaeaceae 5-16 of 114). The gain is DefenseFinder localization bought by splitting universal families.
2. Round 1 -> V0 (+0.067) splits evenly between graph and granularity: gene-level capped edges +0.052
   [+0.033, +0.070] at ~155k tokens; ~155k -> ~300k tokens +0.049 [+0.027, +0.070] on the round-1 graph; applied
   second, each adds +0.016 to +0.018. Ribosomal recovery falls with granularity in every graph.
3. T2 NOT MET in any family, guarded or unguarded. Embedding hierarchies are ruled by their coarsest level: the
   smallest-P choice sends 63-77% of significant instances there, the union of significance extends loci, and DF
   excess falls back to the base level while TXSScan stays at V0's level. Coarse levels keep the ribosomal locus
   (E2/E3/E4/E6 admissible) at -0.074 to -0.114 summed excess against their finest level alone.
4. The one admissible multi-level arm above the base mixes sources: B1u = MMseqs2 id30c80 + e3c50s75 + V0 r30000,
   r19000, r11000: +0.012 [+0.002, +0.022] over the base (criterion +0.02), 1,686 / 114 ribosomal, DF F1 0.849.
5. T3 NOT MET. Hierarchy evidence with no per-query search: embedding levels AP 0.513 (M0 0.370, M2 0.576),
   MMseqs2 levels 0.450, both 0.575 (= M2, -0.001 [-0.012, +0.011]), at 0.16-0.27 CPU-s per query; every
   variant makes 3.3-9.1x M0's unlabelled hits (bound 1.5x), and floors up to 7 and windows up to span + 16 leave
   the rate unchanged. 92-95% of those unlabelled hits carry the query type's DefenseFinder profile domains.
6. T4: the saved Leiden levels are far from nested (32-54% of genes sit in a finer cluster wholly inside one
   coarser cluster). Nesting by intersection changes little: engine -0.001 / -0.003 summed, search -0.007 AP.
7. As built, one hierarchy serves neither job better than one well-chosen flat partition. Its value shows only as
   a hybrid: sequence families carry universal families and TXSScan, fine embedding levels add DF localization.
