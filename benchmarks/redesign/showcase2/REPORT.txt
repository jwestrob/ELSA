==============================================================================================================
5. CAVEATS
==============================================================================================================
- The answer '0' is fixed at triage: every one of the 47 candidates with two eligible replicates has a member that
  resembles a characterised family (or, for rank 4, only unknown-function entries plus a toxin-like structure). The
  folds decide only how the partly recognised blocks behave.
- The unknown-function wording rule reads entry names only; it counts Pfam 'MJ0788 family' and structural-genomics PDB
  titles as characterised. This decides the class of kd_kA_091 / kd_kA_120 (secondary set), and nothing else in the
  fold list. Sharur's family tier carries the same flag (unknown_function False for PF27370).
- Candidate eligibility removed 187 of 227 locus-space candidates before triage, mostly because every eligible
  realisation was annotated, membrane-like (hydrophobic fraction > 0.45, e.g. c928, c917), long, or already folded.
  Hydrophobic units were excluded by the unchanged screen filter and remain untested.
- Kinds replicates are support pairs at whitened cosine >= 0.32 to the passer and 17.5-59.5 % identical to each other
  (median of per-block maxima 26 %); this tests the kind pair across distant homologs (rank 15 replicate 2 fails for the same architecture as rank 4).
- genes.parquet 'length' counts the stop codon in some genomes, so candidate-table lengths can read 1 aa long; fold
  inputs and the design use the protein lengths (composition aa_len).
- Prodigal partial flags are unrecorded for most proteins (treated as unknown, as the census did); the contig-end
  rule still applies.
- The rank-20 rep-1 margin control in the predeclared design used a 78-aa donor for a 39-aa chain (the predeclared
  donor pool held no closer length); two POST HOC length-matched donors give the same reading.
- The biotite Sharur path in ~/.claude/CLAUDE.md (Jacob/Sharur_dbs/DPANN, 3.40 M proteins) predates family_annotations
  and protein_resemblance; context and member annotations used the republished Mac copy (12 Nov 2026 seal), one DuckDB
  thread, read-only.
- Foldseek ran on chains; no complex-level (multimer) search. The 39-42-aa chain of rank 20 produced no alignment in
  either database.
- External services: Biohub API (48 items; sequences); RCSB GraphQL/REST for PDB titles by entry ID (no sequences).
