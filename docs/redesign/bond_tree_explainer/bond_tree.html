ELSA · how conserved loci are called

The bond tree

Two genes get a bond when their matches in other genomes also sit side by side, in more genome groups than chance allows. Bonded genes form a run. The run is cut at its weakest boundary, then each piece the same way. The pieces nest into a tree; each piece is a reported locus.

Neighbor list

A gene's best match in each genome outside its own genome group, kept when the protein-embedding score is z ≥ 10.6.

Bond

Two genes at most 3 positions apart whose matches also lie at most 3 apart in many genome groups. Its strength is Λ.

Bonded run

Consecutive genes with a bond across every boundary. A boundary with no bond ends the run.

Bond tree

The run, cut at its weakest boundary, then each piece the same way. Every piece of 2 or more genes is a node; the nodes are the loci.
