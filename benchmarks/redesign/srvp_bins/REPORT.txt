3. DEREPLICATION

Existing tables used: joint_dereplication_results_v1/cluster_membership.tsv (job 17839; dRep 3.5.0, fastANI, primary
Mash 0.90, secondary 95% ANI, 50% aligned fraction by the 'larger' metric, average linkage, tertiary clustering;
scoring completeness - 5 x contamination + 0.5 x log N50 + centrality). Nothing was re-clustered.
Pick per cluster: the dRep representative when it passes the tier (973 of 994 primary clusters), otherwise the passing
member with the best completeness - 5 x contamination + 0.5 x log10(N50) (21 clusters; 19 Illumina bins and 2 PacBio).
Clusters with any primary member: 994 of 2,392. Average linkage lets a cluster hold pairs below 95% ANI, and the 50%
aligned-fraction cutoff can split incomplete genomes; at >= 90% completeness that split is rare.
