ANSWERS
1. Taxonomy. GTDB r232 has no Methylorubrum: it is part of g__Methylobacterium (146 species representatives).
   Family f__Beijerinckiaceae (NCBI: Methylobacteriaceae), order o__Rhizobiales (NCBI: Hyphomicrobiales), class
   c__Alphaproteobacteria. AM1 itself (RS_GCF_000022685.1) is a member of s__Methylobacterium extorquens; the
   representative is GB_GCA_900234795.1, which carries the full cluster lutA B C D E F G H lanM lutI in AM1 order.
2. The AM1 cluster (Roszczenko-Jasinska et al. 2020, verified in the paper and NCBI CP001510.1): META1_1778-1787 =
   lutA lutB lutC lutD lutE lutF lutG lutH lanM lutI, all on the + strand, 9,864 bp. lanmodulin is META1_1786, inside the
   cluster (gene 9 of 10). Your recollection was right on every point.
3. Main definition (reverse-best component genes, >= 3 distinct components, <= 3 intervening genes): 2,354 loci in 2,170
   of 5,752 Rhizobiales representatives; 584 loci in 530 of 996 Beijerinckiaceae representatives; 1,810 loci in 1,696 of
   15,721 other Alphaproteobacteria; 1,588 loci in 1,551 of 2,338 targeted representatives outside the class (1,547 of
   1,835 GlobDB-proxy-positive; 2 of 500 random proxy-negative). Chance expectation (200 independent per-component
   shifts): 0.505 loci in the order (max 4), 0.22 in the class rest, 0.11 beyond.
4. The cluster has two parts with different distributions.
   (a) A transporter module, lutA/B/E/F (+ often lutG, lutC): the large majority of loci. Order: 2,019 of 2,354 loci
       carry no receptor; class rest: 1,790 of 1,810; beyond the class: 1,502 of 1,588 (Pseudomonadaceae,
       Methylomonadaceae, Oleiphilaceae, Balneatricaceae, Halomonadaceae, Burkholderiaceae_C ...). Commonest
       architectures: order A B E F G (668 loci), A B C E F G (298), A B E F (235); family A B E F (175); class rest
       C E F (356), A B E F G (280); beyond A E F (489), A B E F (263), -C A E F (194).
   (b) The complete AM1-type cluster (lutA-lutI with lutH, lutD, lutI; lanM in 22 of 80): 80 loci, all in Methylobacterium
       (80 of 146 species reps); Methylobacterium has a main locus in 144 of 146 reps (154 loci, 107 with lutH).
       lutD, lanM and lutI homologs inside loci occur only in Rhizobiales (D 146, M 164, I 107 order loci; D 0-1, M 2-4,
       I 0 in the class rest; 0 beyond).
   Receptor + transporter loci without the full set: 239 in the order (Bradyrhizobium 118, Methylocystis 39,
   Methylobacterium 18, BOG-931 18), 13 in the class rest (Acetobacteraceae), 86 beyond (Stutzerimonas 20, Neptuniibacter 9,
   Cupriavidus 8, Rhodanobacter 8).
5. Split arrangements are common: 996 Rhizobiales genomes have a transporter-only locus and a reverse-best lutH homolog
   elsewhere in the genome. Three literature cases reproduce exactly: Beijerinckiaceae bacterium RH AL1 (lutABEFG locus,
   lutH separate), M. aquaticum 22A (B C D E F G locus, separate receptor), M. trichosporium OB3b (M A B E F locus,
   separate lutH homolog).
6. Methanol dehydrogenase (curated KOfam K23995, lanthanide-dependent MDH): present in 1,689 of 2,170 order genomes with a
   main locus and in 94 of 3,582 without (odds ratio 130; genomes are phylogenetically dependent, so the hypergeometric
   p of 1e-860 overstates independence). K23995 lies within 25 genes of 1,087 of 2,354 loci; the commonest flanking KOs
   are K05555 (cyclase), K09733 and K07144 (methanofuran biosynthesis), mxaJ and K23995. In the family, K23995
   sits within 25 genes of only 103 of 584 loci, as in AM1/PA1 where xoxF1 is 35-36 genes upstream of lutA. Class rest:
   K23995 in 980 of 1,696 genomes with a locus vs 338 of 14,025 without (odds ratio 55), within 25 genes of 707 of
   1,810 loci. Beyond the class: K23995 494 of 1,551 vs 63 of 787 (odds ratio 5.4; 161 of 1,588 loci within 25 genes),
   while K00114 (PQQ alcohol dehydrogenase, cytochrome c) is in 1,044 of 1,551 vs 160 of 787 (odds ratio 8.1) and
   within 25 genes of 828 loci. KOfam names the PQQ enzymes; their metal dependence beyond K23995 is UNVERIFIED.
7. Unexpected: the GlobDB r232 per-gene annotation table cannot be joined to the GTDB r232 protein files by gene number.
   GlobDB numbers its own gene calls; its gene index matches the GTDB file order for 3.9% of independent Pfam hits (148,763 hits,
   996 genomes; best offset 0; in single-contig GCF_003173775 the indices agree up to gene ~250 and then drift). Every later use of the
   GlobDB table at gene level needs coordinates (GlobDB gff_cog release, 36 GB) or a direct annotation. Here Pfam and
   KOfam were run directly on the locus genes and flanks with the same profile sets. Within GlobDB's own numbering,
   adjacency is sound: a GlobDB Pfam proxy for the module (PF13458+PF10282+PF00005+PF01061 within 3 genes) recovers the
   sequence calls in the order at sensitivity 0.945 / PPV 0.873 (with PF00593: 0.890 / 0.907).
8. Slice sizes. Family: 996 GTDB reps (4,517,879 proteins) + 412 GlobDB-only genomes. Order: 5,752 GTDB reps
   (27,072,085 proteins) + 2,059 GlobDB-only. GlobDB-only genomes with the proxy module (gap 3): family 97 (23 with the
   receptor domain), order 412 (45). Packages: family complete (universe, FASTA, base + rescue3 tokens). Order: universe,
   FASTA and base tokens (1,275,491 tokens) done; the rescue3 search (1,141,447 query sequences against 26.5 M unique) is
   running in job 92432, projected to finish around 2026-11-14 00:30 UTC.

METHODS
References. AM1 proteins from NCBI efetch fasta_cds_aa of CP001510.1-CP001514.1 (6,234 proteins; ref/AM1_proteome.faa).
  Query set META1_1777-1788 (cluster 1778-1787 plus the two flanking genes, reported but never counted as components).
  Pfam 38.1 GA on the references: lutA PF13458 Peripla_BP_6; lutB PF10282 Lactonase x2 + PF22494; lutE PF00005; lutF
  PF01061/PF12698/PF12679; lutH PF07715 Plug + PF00593; lanM PF13202 EF-hand_5 x3; lutC, lutD, lutG, lutI no Pfam.
Slices. GTDB r232 species representatives (sp_clusters.tsv, rp16_run_r232) with their Prodigal proteomes
  (protein_faa_reps/bacteria): order Rhizobiales (5,752), family Beijerinckiaceae subset (996), class Alphaproteobacteria
  outside the order (15,721; 48,453,653 proteins), and 2,338 representatives outside the class (1,837 with a GlobDB proxy
  run at gap 5, 500 random proxy-negatives, seed 17, three literature controls; 9,286,523 proteins).
Search. MMseqs2 release 18: search refs vs slice, -s 7.5 -e 1e-3 --max-seqs 2,000,000 -a; every hit gene searched back
  against the AM1 proteome (-s 7.5 -e 1e-3 --max-seqs 300). Self bit scores for BSR.
Gene assignment. RB (main and strict): the gene's best AM1 hit is the component and the forward E <= 1e-5. ANY (loose):
  best forward component at E <= 1e-3.
Locus definitions (consecutive component genes on one contig; components counted among lutA-lutI + lanM):
  main   RB, <= 3 intervening genes, >= 3 distinct components
  strict RB, <= 1 intervening gene, >= 5 components including lutH and lutE or lutF
  loose  ANY, <= 5 intervening genes, >= 2 components
Control (chance). Each component's assigned genes shifted by an independent random offset, circular over the genome's gene
  order (contig taken at the new position), loci recalled; 200 replicates; seed 17.
Sensitivity (profiles). For each component, <= 400 RB genes from main order loci (mafft --auto, hmmbuild), hmmsearch of
  every gene within 10 genes of a main or loose locus (E <= 1e-5), components counted within 3 genes of the locus.
Annotation. hmmscan Pfam 38.1 (--cut_ga) and KOfam (ko_list thresholds; full or domain score as kofamscan) on every
  gene of the main loci and 5 flanking genes each side; hmmsearch K23995, K14028, K14029, K00114 (ko_list thresholds)
  over every protein of each slice for positions of methanol-dehydrogenase genes.
GlobDB proxy (all 346,233 GlobDB r232 genomes, Pfam GA rows): letters A PF13458, B PF10282, E PF00005,
  F PF01061/PF12698/PF12679, H PF00593, M PF13202; runs on GlobDB gene_idx with <= 3 or <= 5 intervening genes; ABEF =
  run with A, B, E, F; ABEFH = ABEF + H.
Architecture strings: genes from first to last component of the locus, oriented so that the majority of component
  genes read on +; '.' gene without a component call, '-' opposite strand. Labels A-G lutA-lutG, H lutH, M lanM, I lutI.

CAVEATS
- RB assignment is conservative outside the order: lutA/lutB homologs whose best AM1 hit is a paralog are not counted
  (profile check adds them). Class-rest and beyond numbers are lower bounds for the module.
- Loci near contig ends (17.7% of main order loci) may be incomplete.
- Genome counts are species representatives; associations (MDH) are phylogenetically dependent.
- Functional statements (lanthanide uptake) rest on the AM1/PA1/P. putida literature only: UNVERIFIED for every locus.
