5. DEVIATIONS, CAVEATS, RESOURCES
==============================================================================================================
- Census universe: the dark-family rule moved from the link definition to a column (task instruction to keep filters as
  columns); this adds the R1 category. The screens' universe is the 9,605 dark-family types.
- The tier searched permissive-e3c50s75 families; 2,015 rescue3 chain families have no searched counterpart (1,832
  because the counterpart has < 5 genomes). Their blocks (U0, 2,901 types, 65 foldable) were not folded.
- 'no_hit' rests on family_only alignments (the tier has no UniRef30-enriched search and no hhblits_uniref rows); the
  enriched triage was run on the hit families only.
- Replicate selection maximised taxonomic distance without a sequence-identity check; for 12 blocks it picked identical
  or near-identical sequences. The post-hoc independent-variant fold (3 items) is outside the predeclared design.
- Control donors are forbidden to share a token, but homologous families split across tokens can still be swapped in
  (rank 26's class-level control used the B-variant family 247277; A bound it at .83). Promiscuity by the screen-2
  wording (any control of the interface >= .6): rank 3 BC (.72), rank 26 AB (.83, homologous donor).
- Engine instances use the rescue3 tokens; screens 1-2 used e3c50s75 tokens (token ids preserved; mapping by genes).
- Biohub items (ledger work/s3/biohub_ledger.json): 462 = 1 payload check + 435 screen + 22 follow-up (6 margin
  controls, 16 monomers) + 3 post-hoc (rank-36 independent variant and 2 swaps) + 1 probe (one large item resubmitted to
  test the queue). Budget 2,500 total / 2,000 per 24 h.
- Slurm (-A banfield -p standard --qos=standard_expand -c 24, exclude list, no --mem/--time): 83089 Foldseek + MMseqs2
  split-gene (1:17), 83090 enriched triage of 9 families (8:33). Census (634 s), design (68 s), triage FASTAs and small
  lookups ran serially on the login node (<= 2 threads). Mac: DuckDB export (14 s, one thread), API submission and
  polling, second-scale pandas; structures moved to biotite as they arrived (local fold directories hold metrics only).
- External services: Biohub API (approved); NCBI E-utilities assembly summaries for 3 public accessions (no sequence).
