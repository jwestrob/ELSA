SLOT_RESCUE: VERIFICATION OF THE 175,729 PROPOSED DPANN LABELS (2026-11-21 UTC)
================================================================================
Written by the coordinating session from its own inspection (inspect/insp1-4.py) and from the two checks the
slot_rescue agent set up (verify/; predeclaration verify/predeclared_verify.json, sha256 72d13c1b..., written before
either check ran). The pass table (pass/dpann_slot_rescue_pass.parquet) is unchanged. Nothing is loaded into Sharur.

BOTTOM LINE
1. The groups of related genes are real. A labeled gene has the same fold as its annotated partner in 87.4% of 223
   pairs where both predicted structures are confident (81.9% of all 298 pairs); mismatched-label control pairs 1.0%
   (2.7%). This holds at every support level (81.0% even where under 5% of partners carry the label).
2. The NAME is only as good as the share of partners that carry it. The call counts annotated partners alone, so a
   handful of annotated genes can name a family of hundreds. `vote_share` and the 98.85% calibration do not see this.
3. Support = votes_for / supporting_genomes is the measure that tracks correctness:

INSPECTION (Sharur DPANN database, read-only)
- Label voters as a share of supporting genomes: under 5% for 45,604 rows (median 4 voters among 441 supporters).
- 30 labels (18,483 rows) are proposed for at least 10x as many genes as carry that annotation anywhere in DPANN:
  GM_CSF 1,626 rows from 2 annotated proteins (score 27.4, E 1e-7), Transcrip_act 1,576 from 3, Mis14 1,811 from 12,
  LIM 1,456 from 17, UBA_3 414 from 3, Med9 209 from 5, BcsB 470 from 9. 154 labels (70,086 rows) exceed their
  annotated count. The same regime holds real rescues (Ribosomal_S26e 873 from 276; the tested eL39 leads).
- CDD best hits (protein_resemblance; 86,895 labeled proteins): signal peptidase I 3,067 of 3,168, L14e 1,714 of
  1,719, L39e 262 of 266 agree; LIM-labeled proteins are rpl40e in 440 of 572. HTH labels hold at superfamily level.

  model 54.6%; hits to other label families only 8.0%. Among genes with any hit, the top hit is the proposed family 74.3%.

CHECK B, predicted structures (ESMFold2 through the Biohub API; US-align; 300 labeled genes in three strata, each with
its best annotated partner, and a mismatched-label partner of similar length as control)
- 562 of 564 folds returned. The API never returned JASHSJ010000028.1_8 and MEK6933182.1; after an hour the
  coordinator wrote folds/summary.tsv from the 562 saved results and ended the folding step. 596 of 600 pairs scored.
- TM-score >= 0.5 (larger normalization): own partner 81.9% of 298, control 2.7% of 298. Both pLDDT >= 70: 87.4% of
  223 against 1.0% of 208. Vote share over 0.8: 92.1% of 139; at or below 0.8: 79.8% of 84.
- GM_CSF-labeled genes match their GM_CSF-annotated partner in 7 of 7 pairs: the check confirms homology to the
  partner and cannot detect a wrong partner name. Ribosomal_L39 0 of 4 (about 50 aa; median TM 0.34).
- The driver's own summary table is empty: pandas read the pair types "true" / "null" as a boolean and a missing
  value. inspect/insp4.py reads them as text; the numbers above come from it.

WHAT A USABLE TABLE NEEDS
- A support column (votes_for / supporting_genomes) and each label's annotated count in DPANN.
- A name only for T1 and T2 (84,273 genes); T2b flagged as weaker; "conserved unannotated family" for the rest, with
  the contradicted rows flagged. Or the same thing as a rule in the call: count unannotated partners in the vote.
- For families whose few annotated members carry an implausible Pfam name, CDD names across the family are a better
  source (LIM -> rpl40e).
