COMPETING FUNCTIONAL READINGS (both UNVERIFIED)
==============================================================================================================
R1. A-B is a substrate-mimic inhibitor of C: B occupies P4-P1 and blocks the Cys; the operon keeps C inactive until
    A-B is released or degraded. Predicts: B's chain leaves the groove at P1 without occupying the primed side (the
    models show the hairpin turn right after Gly-48; checkable by superposition onto a protease entry with a
    primed-side ligand); C copies lacking A-B should be rare or catalytically altered (observed: 2/309 core C lack
    any A/B within 5 genes, both N-truncated); B's Gly-48 carbonyl should sit outside a catalytic attack geometry.
R2. B is a cleaved substrate: C cuts B after the invariant Gly-48 (a Gly-|-Ser/Asn/Asp bond), e.g. to release or
    activate A-B. Predicts: C's S1 pocket fits a Gly (checkable from the residues aligned to the S1 pockets of
    1cvr/8dgz); the Gly-48 carbonyl oxygen sits in C's oxyanion hole and the Gly48-col50 bond spans the Cys SG at
    <= 3.5 A (measurable now in the 11 + 24 models); the Algoriphagus B-C fusion should behave as an autoprocessing
    protease.
Either reading needs C's catalytic pair to be intact: His/Cys conservation 98/96 % (DPANN) and 36/38 (public).
