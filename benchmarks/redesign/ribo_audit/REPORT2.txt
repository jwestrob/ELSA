7. CAVEATS
- Operon calls come from strand and spacing; no transcript data exist for these genomes. Spacer-only units (same
  strand, 150-560 nt) are the uncertain calls and are reported both ways.
- 40% of DPANN genes carry neither Pfam nor KO; such genes count as non-members (X) and can neither extend nor join a
  locus by membership (14-15 L4b / Z cut junctions involve one).
- Conserved divergent arrangements (S13|L4e recurring in 12 other groups) are two transcription units by the rule
  applied here and a conserved neighbourhood by recurrence; the verdict follows the operon definition the task gave.
- Literature coverage: two full-text comparative studies (27 and 17+ archaeal genomes, including DPANN representatives
  in T23) plus abstracts; DPANN-specific cluster studies were not found, so lineage-specific members of the DPANN
  superoperon beyond the listed genes count as X.
- Inheritance uses KO / Pfam identity and the lineage-free groups; it is reported, not decisive.
- Login node, one thread throughout: annot 187 s (3.1 GB), verdict 50-80 s, bridge 70-100 s, chance 40-60 s per
  variant. No Slurm job.
