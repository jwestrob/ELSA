1. Cut test on ELSA's rule and families
---------------------------------------
Design (matches the Sharur session's design, with families of >= 3 members):
- Complete = gene caller partial flag '00'. The canonical database carries proteins.partial for 1,502,048 of the
  3,585,303 universe genes ('00' 1,314,509; '10' 92,550; '01' 92,522; '11' 2,467); the other 2,083,255 are NULL.
- Positives: 300 per stratum of family size {3-4, 5-9, 10-99, 100-999, >= 1000} x protein length {< 150, 150-299,
  300-599, >= 600 aa}, 6,000 in total (every stratum had >= 300 candidates).
- Negatives: 500 complete base singletons per length bin, 2,000 in total. Seed 17.
- Cut: N- or C-terminal piece kept (50/50), kept fraction ~ U(0.15, 0.95), at least 30 aa; proteins < 34 aa skipped.
  Fragment length q10 / q50 / q90 = 34 / 142 / 522 aa.
- Search: the genome-wide settings (MMseqs2 18, -s 7.5 --max-seqs 1000, align -e 1e-5 -a).
- The intact original was removed from the targets. 4,732 hits to a sequence carried by no other gene were dropped;
  3,254 hits to a sequence shared with other genes were kept with the remaining genes' token. Family sizes were
  counted without the original.
- Rule (rescue3's hit rule): best hit at E <= 1e-5 whose alignment covers >= 80% of the shorter protein, in a family
  of >= 2 members.

Reading: false joins grow with fragment and protein length (4.7% under 75 aa, 26.2% at >= 300 aa). This is the
pattern expected when a long fragment covers most of a short, single-domain target. Requiring the fragment to be the
shorter protein halves the long-fragment false joins; terminus anchoring removes most of the rest. Wrong-family joins
concentrate in small families (3-9 members) and long proteins.

Exploratory diagnostic (after the predeclared rates; does not set the default). Each intact original was aligned to
the target its fragment joined (676 explicit pairs, align -e 1e-5 -a).
- Wrong-family joins where the intact original aligns to the joined target over >= 80% of both proteins (a sibling
  token of the original's own homologs): rescue3 103 / 301, a 111 / 271, c 111 / 218. The rest are partial-domain or
  no-hit relationships.
- Wrong joins into a different family, excluding siblings: rescue3 3.3%, a 2.7%, c 1.8% of positives.
- False joins excluding a full-length match of the intact singleton: rescue3 12.4%, a 7.9%, c 3.5%.
- Under this reading a passes both bounds (2.7% and 7.9%). The predeclared count includes sibling-token joins,
  because they still place the fragment in a token other than its family's, so the predeclared verdict stands.
