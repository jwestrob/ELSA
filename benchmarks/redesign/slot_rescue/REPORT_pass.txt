DIFFERENCES FROM THE MESSAGE
- 'Total votes' is given as voters_annotated (supporting partners carrying any Pfam / KO; vote_share uses it) beside
  supporting_genomes (all supporters, annotated or not). The runner-up is the top key among supporters that do not
  carry the proposed key; keys that co-occur on the same supporters with an equal count are listed in tied_keys.
- The call for a gene of unknown family is the tested vote() with an empty right-family set (the test merged a known
  family's Pfam and KO into one vote); check a2 shows the two forms agree on every test row.
- 2,798 proteins carry Pfam rows stored by name only (PQQ_3, TYW2_N_arc, Ribosomal_L2, DUF4162, ...). They have a Pfam
  row, so they are outside the candidates and outside the calibration pool; as in the test, they cast no votes as
  partners.
- The confidence table has four sparse cells (< 30 calibration calls); their rates are shown and flagged.
- locus_id uses ELSA DPANN gid intervals of Z L4b+ theta .02 ('Z_L4bp_t0.02:s-e').
