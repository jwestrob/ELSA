2026-11-12 13:10  Design decisions recorded before any result:
  - Negatives are in-chunk pairs of the same genome (> 50 genes apart or different contigs); every
    chunk holds 128 positive pairs of one genome, so the genome accent cannot separate positives from
    negatives inside a batch; it can only shift whole genomes at evaluation (the accent ablation tests
    this).
  - Class-balanced logistic loss -> optimal logit = PMI (density ratio adjacent / product of marginals).
  - Opposite-strand pairs: random order in training, mean of both orders in scoring. Strand never an input.
  - Whitening (soft-ZCA 0.1) is fitted per split on training-side genes only; genome-mean removal is per
    genome and label-free.
  - Validation orders: order-grouped ~18-30% of the training side (seeded); used for early stopping and
    for choosing among the six configurations. Baseline (i) uses the whole training side (train + val),
    which favours the baseline.
  - Folds: orders assigned greedily to the fold with the fewest genes (largest order first). The two
    largest orders (Woesearchaeales 921k genes, Micrarchaeales 681k) each dominate one fold.
  Code: prep.py, train.py, run_prep.sbatch, run_train.sbatch (biotite $RD/grammar/code/).
