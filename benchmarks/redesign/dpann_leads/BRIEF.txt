WHY THIS EXISTS
A position-based call ("slot call") proposed a family label for 175,729 DPANN genes that standard annotation left
blank: a gene inside a conserved locus takes the plurality Pfam / KO among its list partners in other genomes that
sit in the matching position. Verification showed the FAMILIES are real and the NAMES are uneven. Jacob (the PI, an
expert computational biologist) looked at the first examples and called them "fantastically uninteresting". His two
criteria for an interesting lead, in his words:
  A. "things that we can study"
  B. "have some sort of functional assignment we can actually infer", as opposed to "look at this thing we just gave
     a label to".
What fails A: a gene whose function depends on a multi-component assembly with unknown partners and stoichiometry
(he will reject it on sight; it cannot be folded as a defined unit). What fails B: a family with a label and nothing
else behind it, and routine housekeeping genes any annotator would name from context.
What passes: a protein that folds as a defined unit (single soluble chain, or a homo-oligomer / small complex whose
composition is known from characterized relatives), with a specific molecular function supported by independent
evidence, where the assignment changes what we believe about the organism. He said this could be "what we have been
needing for the paper all along". The paper is a statistical method for conserved gene neighborhoods that uses
protein-embedding neighbor lists; a lead that the embedding lists call and alignment lists miss is worth the most.

EVIDENCE LADDER (report each rung with numbers; a lead is SUPPORTED only with rungs 2-5)
  1. Slot call: support = votes_for / supporting_genomes; label amplification = labeled genes / annotated carriers.
  2. The gene's own weak profile hit to the proposed family (tier T1), with model and sequence coverage.
  3. Catalytic and substrate-binding residues of a characterized relative are present at the aligned positions.
     Take residue annotations from a curated public record fetched by accession, and cite the accession.
  4. The predicted structure superposes on an experimental structure of the family, with those residues in place.
     Positive control: a DPANN protein annotated at the gathering threshold for the family. Negative control: a
     DPANN protein of similar length from an unrelated family. State both control results.
  5. Genome context: the function is absent from the genome by every annotation source in the database, including
     non-homologous enzymes that do the same reaction; the neighboring pathway steps are present; the genome is
     complete enough for absence to mean something (use bins.completeness; compare against the clade's rate).
  6. Distribution across clades from bins.taxonomy, with the clade's total genome count as denominator.
  7. Whether the alignment-list version of the call gives the same label (pass columns alignment_label,
     alignment_lists_same_label). Report embedding-only versus both.
  Criterion A check: fraction of ILVFMAWC (about 0.37 soluble, about 0.56 membrane), length, oligomer state of the
  characterized relative, and where it adds information a co-fold with the substrate or cofactor.
KILL a lead plainly when a rung fails. A killed lead with the reason is a good result. Do not rescue it.

NAMING RULE (strict)
Separate OBSERVED (raw hits, residues, superpositions, counts) from NAMED (a function, system, pathway). A slot call
is a hypothesis from bonded partners: write "consistent with". A structure match is never a name by itself. Before
naming anything, inspect the live schema to see which curated annotation resources exist and what they emitted; raw
per-domain tables are observations. Tag every inference that lacks rungs 2-5 as UNVERIFIED.
