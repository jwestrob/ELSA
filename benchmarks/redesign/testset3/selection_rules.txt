testset3 — selection rules, predeclared 2026-11-19T10:38:35Z (seed 17), written before any protein or annotation content of any
candidate is read. Only GTDB r232 metadata (assembly level, CheckM2, contig count, taxonomy, protein count) and the
exclusion lists below are used to select.

Source: GTDB r232 metadata, bac120_metadata.tsv.gz and ar53_metadata.tsv.gz (downloaded 2026-06-29,
  giant_protein_folding/rp16_phylogeny_2026_06/gtdb_giant_scan/meta/), rows with gtdb_representative = t, kept only when
  the representative's proteome exists in gtdb_giant_scan/reps/protein_faa_reps/{bacteria,archaea} (the files used for
  the lut slice). Taxonomy: gtdb_taxonomy of that row.
Quality: CheckM2 columns of the metadata (checkm2_completeness, checkm2_contamination), GTDB r232's quality estimates.
Tier "complete": ncbi_assembly_level = "Complete Genome", completeness >= 95, contamination <= 5.
Tier "fill" (archaea only, used only if the complete tier gives fewer than 300 archaea): not "Complete Genome",
  completeness >= 98, contamination <= 2, contig_count <= 10.
Exclusions, matched on the 9-digit GCA/GCF accession number (so GCA/GCF twins and versions match):
  - lut test set package_k8 ($RD/slice_lut/testset/package_k8/selection.tsv, 1,032 genomes)
  - DPANN development universe ($RD/ws_dpann/package/universe/genomes.parquet, 3,564 genomes)
  - Spacedust collection ($RD/universe/genomes.parquet, 1,317 genomes = 9 query + 1,308 reference)
  - every representative whose GTDB species is Escherichia coli or Bacillus subtilis, including GTDB splits named
    "Escherichia coli_<suffix>" or "Bacillus subtilis_<suffix>"
Orders (all from SHA-256 hex, ascending):
  family order          SHA-256("17:family:" + family name)
  genus order           SHA-256("17:genus:" + genus name)
  genome order          tier complete: SHA-256("17:" + accession); tier fill: completeness desc, contamination asc,
                        contig_count asc, then SHA-256("17:" + accession)
  picking order inside a family: round-robin over its genera in genus order (first genome of each genus, then the second
  of each, ...), so distinct genera come first, then distinct species (every representative is its own species).
Per domain (Bacteria target 700, phylum cap 105 = 15%; Archaea target 300, cap 45 = 15%): walk families in family order;
  from each family take genomes in picking order, at most 4 per family, skipping a genome whose phylum already holds the
  cap; stop as soon as the target is reached (the family in progress may contribute fewer than 4).
Archaea: the complete tier is walked first under these caps (this is "take all eligible" under the caps). If it gives
  fewer than 300, the fill tier is walked in the same family order with the same caps, the 4-per-family limit counting
  genomes of both tiers, until 300 or exhaustion. Bacteria get no fill tier; a shortfall is reported.
DPANN lineages: archaeal phyla present in the DPANN development universe (its genomes.parquet phylum column).
Split (as package_k8): genome_code = rank of genome accession in the package; odd = tuning, even = test.
