GAPSCREEN: ENZYME CALLS THAT FILL GAPS IN DPANN METABOLISM (workstream gapscreen, 2026-11-21 UTC)
=================================================================================================
BOTTOM LINE
- The screen rediscovers all three known families and finds one new lead that passes rungs 2-5 and changes what a
  clade can do: a dihydroorotase-like enzyme (PyrC family, label Amidohydro_1) in 18 Micrarchaeales genomes that carry
  ATCase (17/17) and DHODH (17/17) and no dihydroorotase by any annotation source (rate among the other 808 Micrarchaeales
  genomes 0.000). Its Zn site matches E. coli PyrC residue for residue and its predicted fold superposes on 1J79
  (TM 0.79 / 0.76).
- Second SUPPORTED lead: beta-carbonic anhydrase in Micrarchaeales (Zn ligands 4/4; 17 of 18 calls embedding-only).
  Third: ADP-dependent L-serine kinase in Anstonellales (SUPPORTED, low novelty: 79/101 genes already carry the KO).
  KILLED: small alarmone synthetase (catalytic Mg / ATP residues absent). WEAK: GCS2 ligase (no curated residues).
- Global: 31,558 genome-by-function gaps filled, against 28,639 with functions shuffled within each order. The gap
  count by itself reflects how rarely DPANN genes carry KOs. Gaps inside an otherwise present KEGG pathway are 34
  (null 16.8, 95th percentile 20.2); at >= 50% of the other steps present, 197 (null 93.8, 95th percentile 108.5).
- The coordinator's step 1 (function from partners' KOs) alone misses rpiB and HMGCS: their partners carry no KO
  (0 KO-annotated carriers in 287 and 17 genes). A label-level fallback (KO distribution of all DPANN carriers of the
  label) recovers them. Both function sources are reported.

STEP 1: GENE-LEVEL FUNCTION (code/partner_ko.py, Slurm 119697 smoke 32 s + 119698 1 min 28 s, 24 CPUs; code/label_ko.py)
- Supporters recomputed with the pass code: 175,729 / 175,729 calls reproduced, bond gate 0 mismatches.
- Partner function = plurality KO among supporting partners that carried the winning label; STRONG at >= 3 partners and
  share >= 0.70 of the KO-annotated carriers. 54,551 genes have >= 1 KO-annotated carrier; 46,697 are STRONG (733 KOs).
- Label fallback (only where partners give no strong function): top KO among all DPANN proteins with a row for the
  label, >= 5 KO-annotated carriers and share >= 0.80: 38,261 genes; KO labels taken as given: 858. Total 85,816 genes
  (48.8% of 175,729), 17,672 of them enzymes with an EC.
- Calibration against the gene's own KO: partner function 12,477 / 12,508 (99.75%); label fallback 92 / 119 (77.3%);
  KO labels 86 / 87.

STEPS 2-4: GAPS, CONTEXT, CONTROLS (code/screen.py; KEGG modules and KO-EC from Sharur's local KEGG snapshot,
ko 2026/09/24, module 2026/09/11, built from KEGG by sharur setup-kegg; read only)
- Gap = the genome holds no gene with the KO (kofam / kegg), no KO sharing a complete EC number, no satisfied alternative
  for any module step containing the KO, and no Pfam row for the label (accession or name form). Context = share of the
  module's other top-level steps satisfied by annotated KOs; otherwise present = >= 2 other steps and share >= 0.8.
- Gaps: 34,840 genes, 31,558 genome x function pairs; 5,845 are enzymes; 1,017 sit in a KEGG module; 34 inside an
  otherwise present pathway (197 at >= 0.5).
  by tier (gaps / in pathway): T1 13,838 / 20; T2 4,453 / 11; T2b 3,862 / 0; contradicted 1,833 / 1; rest 7,572 / 2
  by list type: embedding-only 8,967 / 16; both lists 22,591 / 18. Genomes >= 90% complete: 10,298 / 16.
- Null (KOs and labels shuffled among STRONG genes of the same order, 20 permutations): gaps 28,639; with module 1,015;
  in pathway 16.8 (q95 20.2); at >= 0.5, 93.8 (q95 108.5). Pathway context is 2.0x null and a small absolute number:
  KEGG modules cover 3% of the gaps.
- Completeness control: gap rate 46.4% in genomes < 70% complete, 42.3% at 70-80, 41.6% at 80-90, 39.2% at 90-95,
  34.6% at >= 95. Every worked-up lead's gap genomes sit at the clade's completeness (context.tsv).
- Ranked table: screen_leads.tsv and leads.tsv, 4,919 (KO, order) leads (1,597 enzymes, 2,485 with a gap genome, 13
  with an in-pathway gap). Prefilter for criteria A and B (enzyme, non-housekeeping, ILVFMAWC < 0.45, 100-600 aa,
  >= 3 gap genomes, >= 2 T1 genes): 80 leads, 54 KOs.
- Rediscovery: HMGCS (K01641) rank 3, Micrarchaeales, 17/17 gap genomes, 6 in pathway; thymidylate synthase ThyA
  (K00560) rank 14 and ThyX (K03465) rank 15, Pacearchaeales; rpiB (K01808) rank 38, Micrarchaeales, 228/287 gap.
- New enzyme leads in the prefilter beyond these: dihydroorotase (K01465; Micrarchaeales rank 2, also Woesearchaeales,
  Pacearchaeales, Iainarchaeales), mevalonate-3-phosphate 5-kinase (K18690, rank 4, same pathway as HMGCS), dTMP kinase
  (K00943, rank 16, same pathway as Thy), uridylate kinase, GAR transformylase, glucose-6-phosphate isomerase, IDI-2,
  AMP phosphorylase, pyruvate kinase, OPRT (UNVERIFIED, screen only).
