2. Pooling loses nothing for typing: the Chamfer set kernel adds +0.002 / +0.002, gene order -0.001 / -0.023, strand a
   further -0.006 / -0.022, the search's rank evidence -0.002 / -0.007; codebook and random-feature sketches lose

METHOD
Loci and labels. Gold = curated-caller instances with >= 2 resolved members: DefenseFinder 2,472 (66 types), CRISPRCasTyper
3,764 (29 subtypes after dropping 56 multi-subtype calls), TXSScan 804 (one type; references and clustering only). Label =
caller:type (DefenseFinder system_type, CRISPRCasTyper prediction). Higher level = CRISPRCasTyper roman type; DefenseFinder
family prefix (RM_, Lamassu-, pAgo_, Gao_, CBASS_, BREX_, ...). Engine loci = for each gold instance the DH locus with the
best member F1 (coverage >= 0.5), represented by all genes of its span (6,360 loci).
Representations (all on zca10 vectors except the token baselines):
  J      family-set Jaccard (MMseqs2 rescue3)            MOD   plurality engine module (token_modules)
  MODJ   module-set Jaccard                              MEAN  cosine of the mean gene vector
  CH     symmetric Chamfer (mean best-match cosine)      CHr   Chamfer on the search's evidence log(N_db / rank)
  ALN    local alignment of gene order (cos - 0.14, gap 0.3, both orientations, self-normalised); ALNs adds strand
  SK     soft-codebook sketch: spherical k-means codebook on 1 M odd-genome genes, top-r codewords, softmax(20 cos) x idf,
         summed, cosine (K 4,096 / 32,768 x r 1 / 8; selected K 32,768, r 8)
  RFF    random Fourier features of exp(-2 gamma (1 - cos)), D 8,192, mean, cosine (gamma 0.5 / 2; selected 2)
  LZ, LW (part 5, fitted) affine maps on the pooled vector (identical to a linear map on gene vectors before pooling, plus
         centring): LZ = soft whitening (alpha 0.1) of odd-genome core-locus vectors; LW = LZ then whitening of the
         within-pair difference covariance of 160,212 positive pairs = odd-genome cores in different genome groups,
         mutual 15-NN under MEAN, Chamfer >= 0.5, flank cosine (3 genes per side) < 0.2.
Typing protocol. Leave-one-genome-group-out: references = all instances in other data-derived genome groups (emb_hom
average linkage t 0.75: 642 groups, 583 holding gold; stringency check t 0.70, 156 groups); 1-NN label, ties by majority;
J / MODJ with no shared family count as wrong; MOD predicts the majority label of references with the same module. Odd
genomes select (SK, RFF grids; best and indexable arms), even genomes are the test. Genome bootstrap 2,000 draws, seed 17.
Clustering: kNN-15 graph, Leiden RBConfiguration, resolution chosen by AMI on odd instances. Bootstrap with replacement
inflates AMI (duplicated genomes), so AMI intervals come from half-sampling (50% of genomes without replacement, centred).
Map. Units = the 500,842 rule-H cores of the nested reading (span >= 2). MEAN vectors (the part-1 indexable arm), exact
cosine kNN-15, Leiden at {0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50}; resolution 10 by odd-genome AMI on the 3,104 / 3,056 (even / odd)
cores that are the best-F1 core of a gold instance. Census instances map to the core holding most of their genes.
Uncalled cluster = no gene of any core is a member of any curated-caller instance (any caller, any member count).
Search. Benchmark of rounds 1-3 (1,247 even DefenseFinder queries with targets; labels, relevance and AP from bench.py).
M1z rerun with the stored top-20,000 lists: AP 0.79196 = stored, max |d| 0; M1z matched-rate recall recomputed from its
stored hits: 0.069 / 0.106 / 0.166 at 0.25 / 0.5 / 1 wrong-type hits per query (round 3: 0.069 / 0.106 / 0.166).
Locus index: 650,438 DH locus vectors (cores + neighbourhoods), query = member-gene mean, cosine, own genome excluded,
greedy non-overlap from the top 50,000, <= 10,000 hits. Window arm: every window <= span + 8 genes whose end genes have
query dot product > 0.1, scored exactly by the cosine of its summed vector (per-gene dot products + prefix sums of the
local Gram; median 73,035 candidate genes and 98,965 windows per query). Rates: thresholds on odd queries, recall on even.

CAVEATS
- Gold typing uses curated member genes. With engine spans the locus space loses CRISPR-Cas typing to Jaccard (Table 2);
  typing discovered loci needs delineation that matches the unit.
- CRISPRCasTyper labels are mostly cas_putative calls; how much of the 0.72 subtype ceiling shared by every arm comes
  from label noise was not measured.
- Gold clustering AMI favours few large clusters; the resolutions chosen on odd genomes sit at the grid's low edge.
- The map's resolution is coarse (median 303 cores per cluster): the uncalled clusters are families of loci, and their
  top lists rank housekeeping operons first, as expected (Table 6, first list).
- Search arms carry no null model: AP and matched-rate recall only. The locus index inherits the engine's delineation
  (recall any 0.889 / 0.905 against the per-gene scan's free windows). The window arm removes that ceiling and ranks
  lower (0.731), so delineation is not the limit of the training-free vector.
- Unsupported / unlabelled rates for the vector arms use the top 300 hits per query (classification depth).
- The LW gain in search was not predicted (expected +0.01 to +0.04 in typing, nothing stated for search) and has one
  replicate (one fit, alpha 0.1, thresholds as predeclared). LZ isolates the self-supervised step as its source.
- Bootstrap AMI intervals with replacement are inflated by duplicated genomes; half-sampling intervals are reported.
- Two job submissions broke account rules briefly: 88356 went in when the account held 412 running CPUs including a job
  of mine that had just ended; 88486 went in at 448 and was cancelled at 0 s.
