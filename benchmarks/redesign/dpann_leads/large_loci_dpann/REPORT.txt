BOTTOM LINE
- 34,530 outermost loci hold >= 10 genes (528,702 genes; side count 6-9 genes: 64,271 loci). They group into 462
  locus families of >= 2 loci (30,404 loci; 4,126 singletons); 222 families span >= 3 genome groups, 89 span >= 10.
- Classes of 462 families (amended rule [predeclared]): KNOWN 66 [22], KNOWN-EXTENDED 138 [57], MIXED 103 [185], DARK
  66 [73], COHERENT-UNNAMED 56 [82], MOBILE 33 [43]; with >= 3 groups 53 / 64 / 51 / 21 / 27 / 6. KNOWN families hold
  53% of family loci (16,215 / 30,404), KNOWN + KNOWN-EXTENDED 60%, MOBILE 0.5% (151).
- Plainly: the large conserved loci of DPANN are mostly housekeeping superoperons (ribosomal, RNAP, exosome-proteasome,
  translation factors, Sec/SRP, A-type ATPase), type IV pilus loci and textbook pathways. Of the top 20 non-KNOWN
  families by the predeclared score, a manual reading puts 7 as textbook operons the automatic rule missed (Mbh
  hydrogenase, AglB N-glycosylation, LysW lysine pathway, GatCAB, NAD salvage, gas vesicles, a housekeeping mix), 5 as
  pilus loci, 1 as S-layer glycosylation and 7 as something else. Three stand out:
  1. Family 17 (Micrarchaeota, 599 of 1,146 genomes; 547 of 826 Micrarchaeales; 36 groups, 6 orders; 16 genes, 67%
     unannotated): a locus anchored on the genome's only topo VI B carries the architecture of the open A2M-like lead
     c25065 (~244-aa wHTH, ~50-aa protein, ~795-aa A2M-like chain) and supplies its partner list: a DUF87 (HerA/VirB4-
     family) ATPase, a 277-aa 2-TM protein, an 8-TM 374-aa protein and three small proteins.
  2. Family 54 (Woesearchaeales 61/704, Pacearchaeales 44/487; 122 loci, 53 groups): a MoxR AAA+ / DUF58-VWA /
     BatA-type VWA module beside Ig- and cadherin-fold surface proteins, an M36-type metallopeptidase and Laminin_G_3
     giants (median 2,267 aa, up to 9,660 aa) in 42 loci.
  3. Family 29 (Pacearchaeales 237/487 + SCGC-AAA011-G17 103/206; 82 groups): three conserved unannotated genes beside
     the Orc1/Cdc6-PolD replication-origin module; one folds as a SecB-like chaperone (TM 0.52-0.56, E 4e-5 to the
     M. tuberculosis Rv1957 SecB-like chaperone), the other two as winged-HTH regulators.
  Every function name above is "consistent with" and UNVERIFIED (no curated caller covers these loci).
- Grouping: the predeclared rule chained (one component of 28,829 loci) and failed; amended rule v2 (reciprocal list
  partners, collinear chains, Louvain) passes both purity bars (ribosomal 0.998, systems 0.951) and misses both
  concentration bars (ribosomal largest family 0.729, T4P 0.339). Shuffled null: 0 edges, 0 families, both rules.

F17 Micrarchaeota topo VI B / A2M-like / DUF87 locus. Distribution: Micrarchaeales 547/826, Anstonellales 39/200,
  Fermentimicrarchaeales 7/14, JAKAKT01 4/6; 0 outside Micrarchaeota. Composition: topo VI B (the genome's only copy in
  474 of 599 genomes; topo VI A sits elsewhere in 528/528), KRR1-type KH protein, a ~795-aa protein in 560/611 loci
  (adjacent to topo VI B in 467; 1 TM helix in 390/621, hyd 0.40; = the c25065 A2M-like chain), a 244-aa winged-HTH
  protein (family HHsearch CdvB_HTH, prob 97, 73/570 genes), DUF87 HerA/VirB4-family ATPase (504 aa, 95% of members),
  277-aa 2-TM protein (family HHsearch CedB_2nd, prob 93, only 57/1,105 genes), 374-aa 8-10 TM protein (hyd 0.55,
  membrane, not folded), 132-aa 2-TM, 102-aa and 49-aa proteins, ArsR-type regulator. Consistent with a membrane
  machine with a VirB4/HerA-type motor (as in Ced DNA import or T4SS-like transfer) carrying a large anchored A2M-like
  protein; UNVERIFIED. Unexplained: the 277, 132, 102, 49-aa proteins fold confidently (pLDDT 67-90) with no PDB match.
  Distinct from the nearest known systems: VirB6/TrbL, VirB8, VirB11 and T2SSF rows 0/611 loci, pilin rows 6/611, plus
  an A2M-like cargo. Identity with c25065 rests on architecture (protein IDs of the v6 cluster were not mapped). Intact:
  314/611 members have >= 3 flanking genes on both sides; core presence is the same on intact members (0.54-0.96).
  Decisive analysis: profile the DUF87 ATPase and the 277-aa protein against CedA/CedB and VirB4/VirB8 references
  with residue checks of the Walker motifs, and test the A2M-like chain for the thioester motif; experimentally,
  localize the A2M-like protein (surface vs cytoplasm) in a Micrarchaeota co-culture.
F54 MoxR-VWA module with giant surface proteins. Woesearchaeales 61/704, Pacearchaeales 44/487, QMZP01 8/62,
  SCGC-AAA011-G17 6/206, Aenigmatarchaeota 8/315. Core: 654-aa protein (M36-type metallopeptidase CDD hit; folds as
  Ig/cadherin repeats, E 8e-18 to titin I65-I70), 393-aa CARDB/BsuPI protein (Ig-like, pLDDT 94), 140-aa adhesin-like
  domain (TM 0.44 to DraE/AfaE), MoxR AAA+ (336 aa), DUF58-VWA (298 aa), BatA-type VWA (312 aa, 3 TM), 74-aa small
  protein; Laminin_G_3 giants in 42 loci (48 with +-5 flanks). Consistent with a MoxR-VWA chaperone/insertion module
  dedicated to secreted adhesins; UNVERIFIED. Intact 50/122; largest protein per locus median 1,382 aa. Nearest known:
  bacterial MoxR-VWA (BatABCDE aerotolerance) modules, which carry no surface-protein block. Decisive analysis: VWA MIDAS
  motif check (DxSxS...T...D) in BatA/DUF58 and co-occurrence of the module with Laminin_G_3 giants genome-wide.
F29 replication-origin locus. Pacearchaeales 237/487, SCGC-AAA011-G17 103/206, 7 more orders with 1-5. Core: Orc1/Cdc6
  (403 aa), PolD DP1 (527 aa) and DP2 (1,156 aa), PRC-barrel protein, TctA permease (398 aa, 11 TM), and three
  unannotated genes: 212-aa winged HTH (folds to AbfR/MepR/ST1710, E 2e-4; family HHsearch TubR-like PDB hit in 3 genes),
  89-aa MarR/PadR-type wHTH (TM 0.66-0.73, E 1e-4), 142-aa SecB-like chaperone fold (TM 0.52-0.56 to Rv1957, qcov 0.94,
  both representatives). SecB is a bacterial chaperone, so a SecB-like fold beside an archaeal origin is the unexplained
  item; Rv1957 belongs to the HigBA-SecB toxin-antitoxin-chaperone (TAC) system, and the loci carry no HigB/RelE-type
    toxin row (integrase rows in 29 loci). UNVERIFIED. Intact 183/358. Decisive analysis: chaperone-addiction (ChAD)
  motif search in the two wHTH proteins and a co-fold of the SecB-like protein with each (mismatched-pair control).
