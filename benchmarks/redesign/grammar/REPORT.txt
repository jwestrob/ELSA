grammar workstream: REPORT (2026-11-12, DPANN, ESM++ small, rescue3 families)
==================================================================================

QUESTION
Does a small critic s(a,b) = u(x_a) . v(x_b), trained to tell adjacent gene pairs from far pairs of the same
genome (class-balanced logistic loss, so the logit estimates neighbourhood PMI), identify functionally coupled
neighbours on GTDB orders it never saw better than token-pair PMI estimated from the same training orders, most
of all for rare families?

ANSWER
1. The critic learns a transferable neighbourhood signal. On held-out orders it ranks curated-system adjacencies
   above other adjacencies better than PMI from the training orders for all three callers (DefenseFinder 0.824 vs
   0.785, TXSScan 0.852 vs 0.811, CRISPRCasTyper 0.685 vs 0.643), and the gain sits where the counts have no data:
   token pairs never co-located in the training orders 0.768 vs 0.544, singleton families 0.786 vs 0.500; where a
   pair recurs in 1-4 training genomes the two are equal (0.801 vs 0.802).
2. Predeclared: G1 NOT MET (overall +0.039 [+0.029, +0.048] against a +0.05 bar; the never-seen part, +0.224, is
   met). G2 NOT MET (co-folding AUROC 0.692 against the engine's 0.748; the four uncalled co-folders rank 53, 153,
   198 and 211 of 297). G3 MET (shuffled-order critic 0.519).
3. Where a pair has been seen, counts mark system boundaries better than the critic (DefenseFinder against
   straddling neighbours: PMI 0.870, engine 0.845, critic 0.830).
4. The signal is pair-specific. The interaction term alone gives 0.814 and the per-gene term alone 0.290; within
   deciles of raw embedding cosine the never-seen gain holds (0.743 vs 0.547) and the overall gain falls to
   +0.023. Tandem homologs split across families take the highest scores and carry part of the overall gain.
5. Loci read from held-out critic scores are precise and incomplete: summed DefenseFinder + TXSScan excess +0.586
   against +0.444 for the engine default, at lower F1 on every caller, and they fail the ribosomal guard (31 / 114
   Parvarchaeaceae).
6. Beyond paralogs, the top unscored pairs are one recurring type in 28 distinct family pairs the engine does not
   call: a ~50-aa protein whose nearest annotated neighbours carry T4SS_pilin / TrbC / Class_IIIsignal beside a
   longer protein whose neighbours carry SLT / Peptidase_M23 / TPM_phosphatase / PKD (observed domains of
   neighbours; UNVERIFIED as any system).
Reading: the embedding critic complements family counts for lineage-specific pairs with no recurrence evidence;
it does not replace them, and it does not predict co-folding better than the engine.

DESIGN AS RUN
  Input: per-gene L2 residue means (l2_f32__row.npy) minus each genome's mean, soft-ZCA (alpha 0.1) fitted on the
  training side of each split, unit length. Critic towers 960 -> MLP -> d; opposite-strand pairs are trained in
  both orders and scored as the mean of both; strand is never an input. Positives: offset-1 pairs (offsets 1-3 for
  w3). Negatives: in-chunk pairs of the same genome > 50 genes apart or on different contigs (chunks of 128
  positives from one genome, 32 chunks per batch), so every negative is a within-genome shuffle pair.
  Holdout: 5 folds grouped by GTDB order (greedy gene balance); validation = order-grouped 18-30% of the training
  side (early stopping and configuration choice; no gold label enters). Phylum split: train Nanobdellota (1,738
  genomes), score 8 other phyla (1,826 genomes).
  Baseline (i) uses the whole training side (train + validation orders), which favours it.

READINGS AND WHAT WOULD REFUTE THEM
  R1 "The critic transfers neighbourhood PMI to lineages it never saw, where counts are blind." Supported: never-seen
     DefenseFinder +0.224, TXSScan +0.179, CRISPRCasTyper +0.175; phylum split +0.239; interaction term carries it;
     survives cosine binning; shuffled control at chance. Would have been refuted by the gain vanishing within
     cosine bins, by the accent ablation or shuffled control reproducing it, or by the per-gene term carrying it.
  R2 "It replaces counts." Refuted: overall gain +0.039 (below the bar), equal at 1-4 training genomes, behind
     counts on system boundaries (straddle -0.040), and behind the engine on co-folding (-0.055). A stacked score
     (counts where seen, critic where not) is the use this result supports; it was not built here.
  R3 "Part of the score is self-similarity." Supported for the top of the ranking (top-30 raw cosine median 0.952)
     and for about 40% of the overall gain (+0.039 -> +0.023 within cosine bins). Not for the never-seen gain.
  R4 "The critic finds unrecognised coupled pairs." Not established. The co-fold test (G2) fails, and the top
     non-paralogous types are one envelope-like pattern seen in 2-5 genomes of one order each. That pattern is a
     candidate class for a co-fold screen (list above), not a finding.
  R5 Loci: the excess gain is a precision gain at lower F1, not admissible under the ribosomal guard.

DEVIATIONS, BUGS, CAVEATS
  - Bug fixed before the final evaluation: training-only -log P (a sensitivity variant of baseline i) was looked up
    with keys on the global token base while recurrence.colocalization() encodes on (max token present + 1). cv4
    and the phylum split lacked the largest token id in training, so 502 / 364 adjacencies matched. Patched (job
    83502; cv0-cv3 values unchanged to the bit); the primary PMI and the strata never used that path.
  - Six folded 'uncalled' pairs are engine-called under the rescue3 tokens (screen 1 used the pre-rescue base);
    classes are kept as folded.
  - Whitening uses the training side of each split; genome-mean removal uses each genome's own genes (label-free).
  - Pooled CV AUROC mixes five critics; per-fold AUROC agrees in direction (critic leads PMI in 5 / 5 folds).
  - The critic sees offset-1 positives only; strata use window-3 co-location (offset-1 strata in eval1.tsv).
  - CRISPRCasTyper instances are 93% single-member and 99% 'cas_putative'; its rows carry little weight here.
