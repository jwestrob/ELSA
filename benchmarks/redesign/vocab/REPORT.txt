THE ALGORITHM
Input: gene order (contigs of genes) and one label per gene per resolution (stage 1: MMseqs2 rescue3 family; stage 2:
three vocabulary-tree cells of the whitened embedding); a genome -> group array (unit of independence; emb_hom average
linkage theta 0.75, 642 groups, from embedding best-match similarity; no taxonomy).

State. Each contig is a sequence of live nodes. A node is a terminal gene or a word instance (a contiguous span: two
child nodes plus 0-2 inserted genes). Each node carries one label per resolution: a terminal its cells; a word its
projection, the canonical id of the unordered pair of its children's labels at that resolution.

Objective (description length of the group-level occurrence record). For labels X != Y on adjacent live nodes
(<= 2 intervening single genes, either order, either strand), n = groups in which they are adjacent and
   E = sum over groups of [1 - prod over the group's genomes of (1 - min(1, 2w c_X(g) c_Y(g) / m_g))],  w = 3,
c = live copies of the label in genome g, m_g = live nodes of g: the expected number of groups carrying the adjacency
under random order within each genome. Coding the n group occurrences as instances of one word instead of chance
co-placements saves
   Lambda(X, Y) = n ln(n/E) - n + E  nats      (Poisson log-likelihood ratio; ~ n x PMI for n >> E).
Naming a word costs tau nats; the description length sum over words (tau - Lambda) is decreased greedily. A group, not
a genome, is the unit of evidence, so shared descent inside a group is paid for once. n >= 2 is structural (one
occurrence cannot be compressed); labels present in < 2 groups are background.

Updates (one round). (1) Enumerate adjacencies of live nodes (a terminal pair offers every pair of resolutions, so each
slot chooses its radius; a pair involving a word offers same-resolution pairs). (2) n per label pair by sorting
(pair, group) codes. (3) E for pairs with n >= 2 by a merge-join of the two labels' per-genome copy lists, accumulated
per group. (4) Candidates n >= 2 and Lambda >= tau in decreasing Lambda; each occurrence (shortest distance, then
position) claims its nodes and the genes between them; a pair is committed only if its claimed occurrences still give
n >= 2 and Lambda >= tau. (5) Committed occurrences become word nodes; their instances record children and inserted
genes. Rounds repeat until nothing commits (7-21 rounds observed). Output: a parse in which every gene is a slot of one
top-level word, an inserted gene inside one, or background; each instance's merge tree is its nesting.

Stopping rule. tau = smallest value of a fixed ladder whose word-type FDR (mean null types over 3 genome shuffles /
observed types) <= 0.01. No other threshold anywhere.

Readings (no parameters). V-top: top-level word instances. V-nest: + their children that are words. V-mob: + maximal
sub-words whose type also stands alone in >= 2 groups (units whose identity is confirmed by context). V-phr: + phrases,
maximal runs of consecutive words joined through gaps spanned by a round-0 adjacency with n >= 2, Lambda >= tau.

Complexity. Per round O(A log A) for the sorts (A = adjacency-label rows: 10.7 M at round 0 with one resolution,
~9x with three) + O(sum over candidates of min(F_X, F_Y)) for E + O(A_c) for the claims; memory O(A). Linear in genes
up to the log factor; no pairwise genome comparison, no neighbour lists, no reading rule.

Measured run time (biotite, node-64-768g):
  stage 1  one run 64 s on one core, 1.3 GB peak (round 0 ~30 s); 156-run ladder (3 arms x 13 tau x obs + 3 nulls)
           4 min 17 s on 64 processes; readings + scoring 12 s per reading (evaluation).
  stage 2  vocabulary tree 17.5 min on 64 CPUs (16.4 min of it level-1 k-means on single-threaded BLAS; levels 2-3 in
           30 s); one run 414 s on 2 threads, 6.1 GB peak; 32-run ladder 15 min on 64 CPUs.
  (softrec round 2, for scale: 45-103 min from vectors to loci on 64 CPUs.)

FAILURE ANALYSIS
- Exact identity at the word level fragments large conserved neighbourhoods (section 3): a ribosomal superoperon is a
  different composition in each lineage, so no single large word recurs, and the run is cut into 2-4 words. The phrase
  level fixes recovery because it counts junctions by their flanking units instead of by word type.
- Exact identity at the unit level fragments variable systems (CRISPR-Cas: 45% of members never enter a word), and the
  coarse independence unit removes what is left of rare systems. Finer units restore CRISPR-Cas (genome arm .504) and
  let descent back in (-0.20 summed).
- Quantized embedding units do not cure unit fragmentation: family-scale k-means cells split families (ablation), and
  the kind-level cells that do recur coarsen every word. The objective chooses the kind level because it compresses
  best; compression and locus boundaries prefer different resolutions.
- The summed-excess gain is partly a coverage effect (DefenseFinder control .375 vs .439 at equal F1); TXSScan's gain
  is in F1 (+.131) and boundary precision (.80 vs .44).

CAVEATS
- Stage-1 tau* = 10 sits one ladder step above a failing value (tau 8 FDR .0113).
- V-phr was designed after the stage-1 even numbers; its stage-1 scores are post hoc. In stage 2 it was predeclared.
- The vocabulary-tree shape (1,024 / x16 / x8, seed 17) was fixed a priori, not tuned; other trees may behave
  differently. Prototype re-estimation and cosine re-parsing (the alignment-free step of the brief) were not built.
- Typing compares fine word labels with coarse module labels; AMI penalises the fine set. Homogeneity favours words.
- The first two even jobs (88067, 88088) crashed in the typing step after scoring; the reading numbers are identical in
  the rerun (88114).
- Search by parsing a query with the vocabulary was not run this round.
- Login-node work: smoke tests, the full reproduction (63 s, one thread), diag1 (14 s), collect/table (< 10 s).
