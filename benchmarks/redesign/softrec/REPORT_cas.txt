WS_CAS: WHY EMBEDDING-ONLY RECURRENCE SCORES LOWER ON CRISPR-CAS LOCI, AND ONE PREDECLARED FIX (DPANN, 2026-11-13)
=====================================================================================================================
Embedding side: landscape rw1000_e50 (round 2: rank-weighted zca10 lists, depth 1,000, W_e50 >= 1 & L >= 2) read by
round 4's post hoc configuration ('post': instance-graph labels ic:k5o0.3_g1, trimming f 0.8, DH; post hoc in round 4)
and by round 2's selected configuration ('r2sel'). Token side: rescue3 engine, DH. Single outputs (one reading per
output); even genomes = test, odd = tuning; paired genome bootstrap (2,000 draws, seed 17). Diagnosis (Parts 1-4)
uses taxonomy, MMseqs2 families and caller labels freely; the fix (Part 2 of the brief) uses none of them at run time.
Reproduced exactly before any comparison (job 87512): token DH even CCT F1 .479418 and token R1 .425975 (per-instance

ANSWER IN EIGHT LINES
1. The CRISPR-Cas deficit is in the landscape. Even, post vs token DH (-0.0367 over 1,899 loci): loci whose token best
   locus has a boundary without embedding support cost -0.0673 (377 loci; +0.0134 the other way); reading differences
   are net +0.0173 in the embedding's favour. Loci with no embedding support on any member alone cost -0.0530 (209).
2. The rank-weight floor carries 87% of that loss (boundary-level: -0.0584 of -0.0673 even; homolog exclusion -0.0059,
   both -0.0031). Restoring only the 10,581 below-floor token-significant instances within 3 genes of Cas loci gives
   CCT +0.046 [+0.038, +0.054] at no cost elsewhere; the 1,064 excluded ones give +0.008 [+0.005, +0.012].
3. Cas pairs are rare, not special: 30.2% of token-significant Cas-span instances are lost (65% of them token L 2-7),
   and at matched token L and family spread Cas pairs are lost LESS often than other pairs (-0.036 [-0.047, -0.024]).
   Cas loci are hit because they are small and support-sparse: span boundaries supported .544 (token .696; DF .891).
4. The lists hold the Cas homologs: Cas lost-pair genes keep as much of their MMseqs2 family in the top 1,000 as
   matched non-Cas genes (capped share .841 vs .792), and 58% of the token's supporting genera are recovered; the
   best family pair sits at median rank 107 (W_fam median .04), behind ~200 Cas genes of other families per list.
5. Floor: W and count thresholds mark the lost Cas instances only at FDR .09-.98 (W >= 0.25: 34% at .092; L >= 5:
   39% at .43). At fixed L >= 5 the Poisson tail of G given E at depth 1,000 separates them from null (AUC .935 /
   .987 / 1.00 at L 5-7 / 8-15 / 16+; W .60-.76); at L 2-4 nothing separates (AUC .68-.81, reciprocity .73-.80).
6. Fix (predeclared; e selected on odd = 8): second tier P <= 1e-8 & L >= 2 at depth 1,000, admitted only in gene
   intervals untouched by tier 1 (115,781 instances, 75,045 island loci, combined FDR .0027). Even vs token DH: MATCHES
   and BEATS by the coordinator's definition: summed +0.027 [+0.010, +0.047], F1 DF +0.080, TXSS -0.015, CCT -0.007
   [-0.016, +0.003]; ribosomal 1,681 / 1,404 / Parv 114. Not round 4's 'beats' (TXSScan below -0.01). Base post hoc.
7. Against the base: CCT +0.030 [+0.025, +0.036] (83 of 209 even 'missed' loci recovered), DF +0.019, summed -0.038
   [-0.045, -0.031], resolved unchanged. Predeclared refuter (b) FIRED: tier-1 loci stay identical, but islands form
   genome-wide and raise every caller's random-placement control (DF +0.041); refuters (a) and (c) held.
8. The same statistic ungated (W >= 1 or P <= 1e-e everywhere; FDR .002-.004) recovers CCT but costs summed -0.11 to
   -0.16 and resolved 1,274-1,376. Remaining CCT gap: 'missed' loci still cost -0.031 (126 of 209 have no support).

TWO-LINE ANSWERS PER QUESTION
Q1 (where the F1 is lost): in loci the embedding landscape does not support (377 even loci, -0.0673), mostly loci with
   no support at all (209, -0.0530); the reading is net positive for the embedding (+0.0173). r2sel and odd agree.
Q2 (pairs): 2,784 lost instances (30.2% of 9,216 token-significant in Cas spans), 64.6% with token L 2-7 (found: 29.6%),
   embedding median L 3, W .009; 649 (23.3%) are homolog-excluded RAMP/effector pairs; Cas pairs are not lost more.
Q3 (lists): homologs are in the lists at control rates or better, at deep ranks (median best family rank 107); Cas
   lists are filled with other-family Cas genes (mean 200 per list); the representation is not the limit.
Q4 (floor): no W or count rule marks them at a usable FDR; the Poisson G-vs-E statistic at depth 1,000 does at L >= 5
   (P <= 1e-4: 49% of lost Cas boundaries at FDR .0002); at L 2-4 no stored or reciprocity statistic separates them.

CAVEATS
- The base configuration (L1 + L4 on the unoriented landscape) was chosen after round 4's even verdict. The fix was
  predeclared and selected on odd genomes, but the MATCHES verdict belongs to base + fix, and the base is post hoc.
- Summed excess falls against the base through the controls: island loci (75,045; genes in loci +9.7%) raise the
  chance that a randomly placed gold pattern overlaps a locus. The fix trades genome coverage for CRISPR-Cas recall.
- Tier-2 instances are unlabelled and the instance graph was not rebuilt; by construction no tier-2 instance shares a
  boundary with tier 1, so tier-1 labels and loci are unchanged (verified). A rebuilt graph would label islands only.
- Diagnosis oracles add or drop instances by MMseqs2 (token) significance and by proximity to gold spans: they bound
  what a landscape change could recover and are not method candidates.
- Part 2 counts use instances with both genes inside a gold span; the boundary-level attribution also covers instances
  that cross the span edge (87 'missed' loci have no lost instance inside the span).
- Per-genus status in the token-support table collapses genera with several supporting instances by the best status.
- Lineage unit: the default genome -> genus array inside the stored statistics (L counts lineages), as in rounds 1-4.
  No lineage-free map was used; the fix adds no new use of taxonomy.
- Even and odd agree on the main quantities (landscape loss -0.067 / -0.068, missed -0.053 / -0.052, fix CCT gain
  +0.030 / +0.030, summed -0.038 / -0.036); Parts 2-4 are descriptive over all genomes.
