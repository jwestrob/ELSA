BOTTOM LINE   VERDICT: WEAK (reaction class inferred; nucleotide open). Criterion A passes.
1. Most specific function supported: a GalT-branch HIT nucleotidyltransferase acting on the glucose-nucleotide pool of
   a GT3 glycogen-synthase locus. The reaction consistent with every observation is NDP-glucose phosphorolysis
   (NDP-Glc + Pi <-> NDP + Glc-1-P), as in ADP-glucose phosphorylase. UNVERIFIED: which nucleotide (ADP vs UDP),
   direction in vivo, activity. Leloir GalT (UDP-Glc + Gal-1-P) is unsupported: no genome has a Gal-1-P source (galK 0/15,
   0/704 Woesearchaeales) and the GalT hexose-1-P subsite is rebuilt.
2. The 15 are the profile-invisible members of a DPANN subfamily ("D": 116 genes, 114 genomes, 7 orders; 98 named only
   by a CDD COG1085 best hit, 1 by Pfam, 17 by nothing). The 15 share a 45-taxon clade (SH-aLRT/UFBoot 100/100) with the
   other 2 unannotated and 26 COG1085-named genes of the 64; they do not form their own clade.

   (115/119 representatives share a 92.6/97 clade with all 15 ADPGP references) and by PANTHER (1,209/1,245). D sits
   outside that clade and outside GalT class I; D replaces the named form (both in 2/114 D genomes).
4. Co-folding confirms a homodimeric NDP-sugar site at the nucleophilic His and cannot separate ADP- from UDP-glucose
   (controls: placement non-discriminating; pair ipTM picks the cognate nucleotide in 3/4).
