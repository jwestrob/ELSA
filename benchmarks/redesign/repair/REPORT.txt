ws_repair: gene-call repair of family tokens on DPANN (12 November 2026)
==========================================================================

Verdict
-------
- Fragment rescue works and passes the predeclared test in both variants. The predeclared tie-break
  (odd-genome summed excess, +0.002 against +0.001) prefers rescue3 (genes in tokens with fewer than 3 members),
  so rescue3 is the adopted repair. On even (held-out) genomes it raises F1 on the 101 gold instances it touches by
  +0.260 [+0.181, +0.340] and their excess over random placement by +0.166 [+0.112, +0.223]. The DefenseFinder +
  TXSScan summed excess moves by +0.004 [-0.003, +0.010], because only 230 of 7,096 instances (3.2%) are touched.
- Split unification (both pieces take the token of the intact homolog) damages the instances it touches:
  F1 -0.308 [-0.391, -0.229], excess -0.253 [-0.331, -0.182] on even genomes. Mechanism: the engine counts only
  pairs of different tokens (recurrence.py colocalization: `ok = ... & (ta != tb)`), so a unified pair loses the
  recurrent adjacency of its two pieces, which is the signal that located it. 168 of the 220 touched instances
  contain both pieces (F1 -0.326); 106 are two-member instances made of exactly that pair (F1 -0.510; 103 of them
  CRISPRCasTyper). Every arm that includes split unification fails c2.
- Every arm is admissible on the ribosomal acceptance check: 1,686 / 1,686 runs and 114 / 114 Parvarchaeaceae.

Predeclared criteria (predeclared.json, written 2026-11-12T07:18:00Z, before any search or engine job)
- c1: DefenseFinder + TXSScan summed excess A - B on even genomes, 95% lower bound > -0.005.
- c2: mean F1 on even-genome gold instances (>= 2 members) with >= 1 member gene whose token changed: A - B >= +0.03,
  paired genome-bootstrap interval excluding 0 (2,000 draws, seed 17).
- Adopt if both pass; among adopted arms, prefer the larger odd-genome summed excess difference.
- Refutation checks (glue, max-seqs truncation, 20-gene inspection) are reported below.

Design (all genes, one parameter set genome-wide)
------------------------------------------------
Queries: unique sequences of every gene in a base token with < 3 members (372,088 uids, 62.5 M aa) and the shorter
gene of every same-strand, distance-1 pair with nucleotide gap <= 50 (1,477,865 pairs, 487,865 overlapping;
867,645 uids); union 1,093,510 uids (197.8 M aa) against all 2,547,528 unique sequences.
MMseqs2 18: prefilter -s 7.5 (the base clustering's sensitivity) --max-seqs 1000; align -e 1e-5 -a; coverage
applied in code. 4 shards on 64 CPUs: prefilter 819-847 s, align 468-474 s, convert 40-62 s (about 23 min wall).
347,122,527 hits; 861,909 queries with a hit. The prefilter reached 1000 for 99.3% of queries; the truncation checks
below show it is harmless for both rules.
- Rescue: hit at E <= 1e-5 covering >= 80% of the shorter protein, in a base token with >= 2 members other than the
  gene's own; best hit by bits. The moves are applied together, and a gene whose best hit itself moved follows it
  (rescue3: 676 chains, 403 cycles; rescue1 cannot chain).
- Split: piece hits E <= 1e-5, piece coverage >= 0.7, region on X >= 80 aa and <= len(X) - 60 (the sizing study's
  definitions), X present in another genome, regions in genomic N-to-C order, overlap <= 20 aa, <= 10 aa of X
  uncovered between them, union >= 0.8 of X. Stage 1 takes candidates from the shorter piece's hits (6,622,765 rows,
  568,596 pairs, top 30 per pair). Stage 2 aligns the longer piece to each candidate on an explicit prefilter
  database (4,593,694 pairs in 22 s; 762,068 at E <= 1e-5). 38,651 pairs qualify (43.7% overlapping); 72,918 genes are
  assigned, of which 44,287 change token (the rest already shared X's token).
- Arms are written to engine/dpann_tokens/ as mmseqs__seq__e3c50s75_<repair>__dpann.parquet (+ .json), all
  3,585,303 gids. The universe is unchanged.

Truncation checks (max-seqs 1000 against the sizing study's search S at max-seqs 8000)

- Split: 3,531 of the 4,056 sizing-study broken-call-like pairs with joint coverage >= 0.8 recovered (87.1%; bar 85%):

Refutation checks
-----------------
- Glue (control rising with F1): the control rises too, by 30-45% of the F1 gain (rescue3 +0.094 against +0.260;
  rescue1 +0.137 against +0.301), and the excess gain holds at +0.16 with intervals above 0. Joining a larger family
  lengthens loci, which partly helps random placements; most of the gain is localization. The largest token grows by
  0.5% (7,675 -> 7,712), and at most 127 genes enter any one token. Refutation not met.
- 20 random rescue1 genes (results/inspect_rescue1.tsv): 17 are the shorter protein (16 of them 34-470 aa, one
  2,413 aa against a 2,681 aa partner; partners 81-2,876 aa; covered 81-100%; identity 0.23-1.00, 8 of 17 >= 0.84),
  i.e. fragments joining their family. 3 have the
  target shorter: 134 vs 124 aa at 31% identity covering both fully (a family member the representative-based
  clustering missed); 211 vs 103 aa at 100% identity (query = target + 108 aa); 1,163 vs 232 aa at 30% identity
  covering 17% of the query (domain-level glue, 1 of 20). Genome-wide, the target is the shorter protein in 14.9% of
  rescue1 moves and 17.8% of rescue3 moves; identity q10 / q50 / q90 0.29 / 0.67 / 1.00.
- Fragments: 2,784 of the 2,786 singleton fragments in sample genomes (sizing-study definition) move; they are 53% of
  the 5,268 moved sample genes.
- Split receiving tokens: the three largest take 3,210 / 2,813 / 2,090 genes from 2,316 / 1,939 / 1,845 genomes
  (median 380 / 101 / 493 aa); 99.5-100% of these genes carry a Pfam hit (annotation_flags). The pieces are
  widespread annotated two-gene states, consistent with the sizing study's clade-wide split families. Domain names
  were not retrieved: the annotations query returned 0 rows despite the flags (unresolved; observation only).

What would let split unification be tested fairly
-------------------------------------------------
Under the current engine a token-unified pair is invisible to itself. Two routes, both without touching the package:
(1) an engine variant that counts same-token adjacency within the window (this also changes tandem duplicates, so
it needs its own base run); (2) a merged-universe run in which each qualifying pair becomes one gene position
(design in merged_universe_design.txt: shadow package with remapped universe and gold, two engine runs, about 5
minutes of compute, no new search). In (2), 106 two-member gold instances made of exactly the pair become
single-member instances and leave the min-2 set, so the comparison must be restricted to instances present in both
universes.
