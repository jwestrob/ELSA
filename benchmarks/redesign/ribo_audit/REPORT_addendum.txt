ANSWER
1. Reproduced exactly (own scorer = ribosomal_check.score run by run): L4b 1,680 recovered / 1,598 resolved (56
   under-covered, 32 over-extended, 0 both; Parvarchaeaceae 114 / 107); L4b+ identical to L4b run by run (same best
   locus for all 1,686 runs); L1 1,680 / 1,036 (53 / 594 / 3; Parvarchaeaceae 114 / 106).
2. L4b's failures are 94.3% [87.4, 97.5] definition artefacts by the corrected rubric (83 of 88: 54 junction splits,
   22 scoring rule, 7 flank), 3 method errors and 2 data. L4b holds the fewest method errors of the six outputs under
   the corrected and strict rubrics (3 and 5 runs; TOK 29 / 64, WCP 48 / 56, EMB 136 / 163, L1 186 / 251). L1's 650 failures are 71.1% definition
   (flank 252, nearby Ribosomal_* 156, junction 54) and 28.6% method (186 extensions into unassociated flank).
3. The gaps in the 56 under-covered L4b runs are lineage-specific junctions: 159 junctions without a bond, none above
   chance in other groups (Lambda 0 for every crossing pair; at most 15 other groups, median 6; 24 in fewer than 3);
   every universal junction inside these runs carries a bond. L4b cuts its best locus at rearranged junctions,
   S3_C|S5_C (23 of 83 cuts) and S17|L22 (20), in Nanobdellota 32, Aenigmatarchaeota 14, Parvarchaeaceae 7 runs.
4. Sensitivity (resolved of 1,686; random-window chance in parentheses): L4b standing 1,598 (2.41%), any locus 1,620
   (2.81%), conserved-flank precision 1,617 (2.52%), + junction split 1,671, + any locus 1,679. L4b leads under the
   standing rule and under every alternative; under the proposed definition (flank + junction split) L4b 1,671,
   WCP 1,622, TOK 1,571, EMB 1,490, L1 1,433.
5. Independence: the flank criterion and the junction split both rest on cross-group recurrence, the signal the
   methods use. An annotation-only test (KO-derived roles, no gene order) backs 9-13% of the conserved-flank gain with
   the narrow translation tier (L4b 0%) and 26-51% with a broad, knowledge-informed tier; strand flips, the one
   recurrence-free sign of a junction break, back 12 of 12 TOK, 12 of 21 WCP, 10 of 20 EMB, 18 of 54 L4b junction
   cases. Requiring independent support for both moves the method share to TOK 40.2%, WCP 59.8%, EMB 56.0%, L4b
   50.0%, L1 79.5%: the definition-artefact shares in REPORT.txt are upper bounds, and WCP's and L4b's depend most on
   the recurrence criteria. In absolute errors L4b stays lowest (44 vs TOK 70, WCP 159, EMB 288, L1 517).

A5. INDEPENDENCE OF THE CRITERIA (the coordinator's caution)
  Both definition corrections that matter use cross-group recurrence: conserved-flank precision (a flank family recurs
  beside runs in >= 3 other groups) and the junction split (a junction recurs in no other group). The methods grow and
  stop loci on the same kind of signal, so these corrections can favour any output built on recurrence. Two checks
  independent of locus calling were available:
  (a) Annotation only. Sharur semantic_terms holds KO-derived role terms (KOfam / KEGG; no gene order). Tier T =
      {ribosomal_protein, translation, translation_factor} (the marker's own function; 227,373 genes = 6.3%). Tier I =
      T + {trna_synthetase, trna_modification, rna_processing, transcription, rna_polymerase, replication, sec_pathway,
      signal_recognition} (354,300 genes = 9.9%); tier I is KNOWLEDGE-INFORMED: it was written after SecY and the
      primase were seen in the flanks.

CAVEATS
- Tier I was chosen after the flank genes were seen; tier T is the only annotation tier fixed by the marker's function.
- KO roles cover a gene only when KOfam assigns a KO above threshold; unannotated genes fail every tier.
- Strand flips miss same-strand rearrangements (S3|S5, S17|L22), so (b) undercounts real lineage-specific junctions.

  over 1,686 runs and 8,325 windows, gap table) 100 s, 0.9 GB. No Slurm job.
