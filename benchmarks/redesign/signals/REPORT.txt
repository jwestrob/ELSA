ANSWER
1. Signal 1 (partner covariation) exists and is specific to direct same-strand neighbours. Median excess E over
   genome similarity and over matched unlinked families: +0.057 [+0.046, +0.069] for 1,000 engine-called operon-like
   pairs (n >= 30), +0.009 [+0.004, +0.016] for adjacent opposite-strand pairs (difference +0.048 [+0.035, +0.061],
   holding at matched engine L), and +0.012 to +0.016 for called pairs 2-3 genes apart (post hoc). Both
   predeclared existence criteria are met.
2. Signal 1 cannot be used to predict co-folding. Where it is defined (168 screen-1 folds, 88 family pairs, 29 hits),
   AUROC is 0.573 [0.423, 0.727], equal to the engine on the same folds (0.575) and below the critic (0.630).
   Combined with the engine it gains +0.028 [-0.105, +0.173]; the predeclared refuter fires. It needs >= 10 distinct
   instances, which only 28% of called pairs and 10 of 297 uncalled folds have.
3. Curated complex vs non-complex pairs: overall AUROC 0.575 [0.467, 0.687] (not met). RM Type I/III complexes vs
   Type II REase+MTase give 0.787 [0.645, 0.910]; CRISPR effector/adaptation give 0.534. The showcase operon is
   present but unexceptional: E +0.14 (A-B), +0.14 (B-C), +0.23 (A-C), permutation p 0.005 (the floor), 71st-83rd
   percentile of operon-like pairs.
4. Signal 2's lineage component exists but is small per gene. Genome means hold 2.2% of family-centred residual
   variance (0.11% expected). The predeclared held-out-family classifier misses both bars (top-1 1.16x majority;
   0.17 bits). It was underfit at 4 epochs; converged (post hoc), it carries 0.75 bits [0.67, 0.83], 68% of what
   the whole vector carries.
5. Per-gene foreignness behaves like provenance (predeclared criterion met). Stratified, even genomes: DefenseFinder
   +0.49 bits [+0.36, +0.63], ribosomal markers -0.24 [-0.28, -0.21], transposase/integrase-domain genes +1.00
   [+0.91, +1.09]. Restriction-modification systems are the most foreign defence types (Type I +1.44). The showcase
   A and B genes score +2.0 and +1.7 (exploratory).
6. Coherence exists (predeclared criteria met): adjacent residual directions agree, +0.038 [+0.037, +0.039] above
   pairs on different contigs and decaying to +0.001 at 50 genes, and curated adjacent member pairs sit +0.10 above
   strand-matched pairs. Post hoc, it is a same-strand transcription-unit signal (offset 1: 0.053 same strand,
   0.0045 opposite) that native ribosomal operons share (0.084 vs 0.040). Coherence measures unit cohesion; a shared
   foreign origin is not established for it.
7. Boundary: for DefenseFinder, coherence separates edges from interiors (AUROC 0.659 [0.636, 0.682], met) but adds
   only +0.012 [+0.007, +0.017] to engine support (bar +0.02, not met). For CRISPR-Cas, where support is weakest,
   it adds +0.043 [+0.016, +0.070]; the label-free variant adds +0.056 [+0.029, +0.082].
8. In the method: signal 1 stays out; it gains nothing where it is defined, and it is undefined where it would be
   needed. Signal 2's coherence can go in label-free (64 genome-background directions, about 1 min, no taxonomy, no
   classifier) as an end-trimming feature of the reading rule aimed at the CRISPR-Cas deficit. Per-gene foreignness
   can go in as an annotation. The next test is a predeclared coherence-based end-trimming rule, judged on
   even-genome F1 with the ribosomal guard.

==============================================================================================================
METHOD
==============================================================================================================
Vectors: ESM++ small residue means with the zca10 transform, recomputed from the softrec code and kept at unit
length. Families: rescue3 MMseqs2 tokens. A gene is eligible when its family has >= 3 distinct sequences (75,890
families; 3,181,891 genes, 88.7%).
- Family-centred residual: r_i = x_i - c_F^(-i), a leave-own-sequence-out centroid over distinct sequences.
- Genome background: b_g = mean r over the genome's eligible genes, used leave-one-out (leave-two-out for an
  instance pair).
- Genome-centred residual: e_i = r_i - b_g^(-i).
Neither signal reads taxonomy; taxonomy appears only as an evaluation label (phylum, order). The lineage inputs
are all data-derived: lineagefree genome similarity (emb_mean) and genome groups (fam_jac 0.2: 232 groups;
emb_hom 0.75: 642; emb_mean 0.753: 1,965).

Signal 1 (s1_partner.py, s1_eval.py)
- Instances of a family pair (F, G): genes at offset 1-3 on one contig; one instance per genome (the closest); one
  per distinct sequence pair. n = number of distinct instances, capped at 400.
- M: partial Spearman correlation, over all instance pairs, between cos(e_F,i, e_F,j) and cos(e_G,i, e_G,j).
  Partialled covariates: a cubic in rank genome similarity, plus same-group indicators at species and family
  scale.
- E: mean over 10 + 10 far background families H of [M(F,G | I_H) - M(F,H | I_H)], plus the G-side mirror. Each H
  has a gene in the same genomes >= 20 genes away or on another contig, and is matched to the replaced partner on
  tree-likeness. E is the mirror-tree signal that remains after genome similarity and after the covariation the
  same family shows with unlinked families in the same genomes.
- Permutation null: partner rows permuted within emb_hom 0.75 groups (199 permutations).
- Secondary statistic: out-of-sample first canonical correlation (genome-parity split).

Signal 2 (s2_classify.py, s2_analyze.py)
- Classifier: a linear softmax maps r_i to the genome's group. Families are split by token parity, and each gene is
  scored by the model trained on the other half (cross-fit). Labels: fam_jac 0.2 (primary); phylum and order for
  evaluation.
- Foreignness: f_i = -log2 p(host group | r_i).
- Coherence: host-relative direction u_i = the centred log-probability vector of gene i minus the mean over the
  genome's other genes; c_ij = cos(u_i, u_j). The boundary score is -c across a gene boundary.
- Engine support: the engine's boundary_support (the maximum L of a significant pair spanning the boundary).
- Label-free variant (post hoc, s2_labelfree.py): u is built from the projection of r onto the top 64 singular
  vectors of the genome-background matrix, which hold 94.5% of its variance.

Compute: biotite, 24-CPU jobs only. No GPU, no language-model inference, no folds, no external service.

==============================================================================================================
CAVEATS
==============================================================================================================
- E is a family-pair statistic and the co-fold label is gene-pair specific. The two replicates of one family pair
  share a score, so intervals resample family pairs. Positives are few (29 hits at n >= 10; 13 at n >= 30).
- What E measures beyond linkage is not identified. Adjacent same-strand pairs exceed opposite-strand and
  2-3-apart pairs, which argues for partner specificity, but co-transcription, co-transfer of operons and
  co-evolution are not separated. The offset strata were not strand-controlled.
- The curated class labels apply literature architecture (stable complex vs separate enzymes or modules) to the
  callers' component calls. Class sizes are small (RM 21 vs 29; CRISPR 46 vs 11).
- The predeclared classifier was underfit; the 30-epoch run is post hoc. Raw foreignness carries the host group's
  prior, and the genome-centred version (post hoc) removes it; DefenseFinder and ribosomal keep their signs and
  sizes.
- fam_jac groups are data-derived from family sharing (no taxonomy). The label-free variant shows coherence needs
  no labels at all.
- Signal 1 has no fitted parameter and was evaluated on all genomes. Signal 2's comparisons report even genomes
  (all-genome values in s2_tests*.json agree). The boundary model was fitted on odd genomes and scored on even.
- Deviation: job 88128 was submitted while the account held 436 CPUs and was cancelled before it started.

   F1 per caller with the ribosomal guard, targeting the CRISPR-Cas deficit (support AUROC 0.596; support + c

   same-strand pairs with >= 50 instances, as in the A-B-C analysis, and judge it on the co-fold tables.
4. To separate expression from origin in coherence, take an expression proxy from nucleotide sequence (codon
   usage of ribosomal-protein genes as the reference set) and repeat S2.3 within expression strata.
