ANOMALY AUDIT (two null checkpoints; no expansion was made; the next tests are listed, not run)
  - Fold-matching candidates carry other residues at the family's invariant positions (gluconate 2-DH gamma Asn
    replaced in 24/24, mostly by D, H, Q, R, S or T), so the labels track the structural family and diverge at the core.
  - The partner-derived KO is shared by >= 70% of label-carrying partners, yet the genes' own relaxed hits name it in
    773/1,015 cases and the 167 remaining genes fail the core: the partner KO may describe the locus more than the gene.
  - Orthogonal tests, each < 1 h on existing files: (a) align each candidate cluster with the annotated members and
    ask whether the candidates share their own invariant set (a sister subfamily); (b) compare each gene's partner KO
    with the KO of its own best relaxed hit at the same locus position in partner genomes; (c) recalibrate the extended
    positives on annotated members at 30-50% identity to the definition set to measure sensitivity on divergent members.
