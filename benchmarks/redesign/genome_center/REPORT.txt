=====================================================================================================================
Outside-review proposal 1, tested on REPORT3's search benchmark (5-fold genome CV, 2,447 DefenseFinder queries in 1,384
genomes; same stored ESM++ small vectors, folds, queries and relevance as REPORT3's M1z). Predeclaration predeclared.json,
sha256 8aa2763cb7e1d359b6e3ff08a9e9a508f5ea86c8a34f68825ae71f7e655d088a, written 2026-11-20 23:29 UTC before any arm was
computed. Paired genome bootstrap (2,000 draws, seed 17) as REPORT3. Every arm is fitted on all 3,585,303 genes, as
REPORT3 fits zca10.

1. The proposal as stated (subtract each genome's own mean, then whiten) is a NEGATIVE result. G1 removes the genome
   means almost completely (0.034% of total variance on genome means afterwards; 0.099% expected by chance) and AP
   stays at Z's value (-0.0001). G05: +0.0003. Removing per-genome offsets buys nothing on this benchmark.
2. Projecting out the leading principal directions of the genome-mean matrix MEETS the criterion: B8 +0.0104, B32
   +0.0118. The gain holds in every fold; the mean gain is 9.5x (B8) and 6.6x (B32) the fold-to-fold SD: per-fold B8 +0.0115 /
   +0.0089 / +0.0101 / +0.0103 / +0.0113 (SD 0.0011, range 0.0026); B32 +0.0129 / +0.0100 / +0.0103 / +0.0118 / +0.0142
   (SD 0.0018, range 0.0042). Every per-fold interval lies above 0. B32 reaches +0.0100 or more in all five folds; B8
   falls to +0.0089 [+0.0074, +0.0106] in fold 1. Every arm chose W + 8, tau 3 in every fold, so the fold values differ
   only by query set. Queries gaining / losing: B8 73% / 21%, B32 66% / 28%. Even and odd halves agree (B8 +0.0100 /
   +0.0108; B32 +0.0113 / +0.0123); >= 3-member queries: B8 +0.0086, B32 +0.0170.
3. The gain generalizes to TXSScan (+0.025 / +0.030 AP) and leaves CRISPR-Cas where it was: CRISPRCasTyper AP +0.003 /
   +0.004, recall at E <= 0.01 unchanged (0.066 -> 0.067), wrong-subtype hits per query 17.38 -> 17.61 / 17.83
   (+0.23 [-0.12, +0.54] / +0.46 [+0.09, +0.82]). The review's CRISPR-Cas argument finds no support here.
4. Reading (interpretation, UNVERIFIED): the gain comes from deleting a few global directions shared by all genes, and
   per-genome offsets are a different thing. The top 8 genome-mean directions carry 94.2% of the between-genome
   variance (top 32: 99.3%) and coincide with the dominant directions of gene-level variance: projecting them out
   lowers the leading covariance eigenvalue from 0.128 to 0.026 (B32: 0.0087) and raises the participation ratio
   from 3.9 to 16.8 (B32: 81.0). G1 widens top-1,000 genome-group breadth more than B8 (+5.1 vs +3.1 groups) with no AP
   gain, so list breadth is not what drives AP.
5. What the top between-genome directions track (protein length, composition, genome GC, completeness) is outside my
   tables; only the spectrum is known (PC1-5: 42.8%, 18.5%, 15.4%, 7.6%, 5.4% of between-genome variance).
6. Cost of one arm through the bond tree and the seven DPANN bars: about 19 A5000 GPU-minutes (z table 12 min measured,
   exact kNN ~7 min scaled) plus about 27 min on one 64-CPU node, about 1 h wall; details in section 6. B8 and B32 are
   global linear maps, so the existing unique-sequence pipeline takes them unchanged.

7. WHAT DIFFERS FROM THE BRIEF
- List depth k: the frozen PRIMARY bond tree has no fixed depth (z >= 10.6, one best match per genome, own group
  excluded; mean ~800 genomes per curated gene on Z). S3a recomputes that list exactly per arm; S3b uses the
  configuration's fixed depth 1,000.
- Same-component genes: the gold tables carry no per-gene component names, so (caller, label, rescue3 family) stands
  in for the component.
- Cas reference 107 is a pair-level statistic; the gene-level best rank asked for sits at 1 in every arm (section 5).
- TXSScan numbers come from the scan (AP needs no null; wrong hits are 0 by construction), as predeclared.
- Z lists against the stored head lists: 0 of 18,414 rows identical, overlap of differing rows >= 0.99935 (median 1.0),
  largest s difference 1.10 (= log 3, near-ties at the top of some rows), from float16 flips after recomputation; the
  predeclared AP gate passed (above), and Z's CRISPRCasTyper null runs equal REPORT3's.
- The first smoke job (119447) stalled for 12 min on one core in kernel page reclaim (node: 9 GB free, 660 GB page
  cache) and was cancelled at 17 min 37 s; the code was rewritten with preallocated buffers, in-place quickselect and
  numba kernels, each checked against its reference (float16 rounding bit-identical to numpy; list kernel identical to
  locus_search.topk_rows, also asserted on 4 rows inside every list job). No predeclared definition changed.
- B8 / B32 directions are fitted on all DPANN genomes, like zca10 (REPORT3's odd-genome zca10 fit changed AP by
  +0.0002); an odd-genome fit of the B directions was not run.
- One adjacent control was not run: projecting out the top k principal directions of the gene-level covariance
  (genome-free) would separate "genome-mean directions" from "dominant variance directions".
