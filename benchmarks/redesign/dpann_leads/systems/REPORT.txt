SYSTEMS WORKSTREAM: CURATED SYSTEM CALLS AS CONTEXT FOR SLOT-LABELED DPANN GENES (2026-11-21)
=================================================================================================
BOTTOM LINE
One lead holds, at system level. The slot call supplies the missing antitoxin of AbiE (AbiEii nucleotidyltransferase
toxin + AbiEi antitoxin, a defined two-protein abortive-infection / toxin-antitoxin system) at 2,319 AbiEii-toxin loci
that DefenseFinder left uncalled, in 1,280 genomes (609 species, 33 orders). The caller's own AbiEi profiles hit
1,127/2,314 of these genes below GA and 1/2,214 length-matched slot genes with other labels; gene order matches the 336
called systems; folds superpose on the Streptococcus agalactiae AbiEi crystal structure as closely as a
GA-annotated DPANN AbiEi. AbiE genomes rise from 290 to 1,377 of 3,564 (RM Type II, the most frequent called
system, sits in 630). The system call stays UNVERIFIED (0 completing genes pass GA): write "components consistent
with AbiE". The gene-level role (antitoxin beside its toxin) is readable from context; the new content is the scale
and the caller-profile support.
Embedding-only share is 149/2,319 loci (6.4%); the alignment lists give the same label for 1,982 (85.5%).
Screen (a), model components missing from called systems, gives 5 gains in 4,495 calls (Type I RM fragments). Six
further candidates are KILLED below. Folds used: 28 of 120 (18 single chains, 10 co-folds).
