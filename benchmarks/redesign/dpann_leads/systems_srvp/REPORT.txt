METHOD NOTES
- The callers deleted their per-protein hmmer output, and single-hit partial loci never reach rejected_candidates.
  Per-protein hits were rebuilt with the callers' own 743 profiles of small multi-gene models (Aksha --cut_ga, whole
  proteome, array 120279) and MacSyFinder's selection (i-Evalue <= 1e-3 at the genome's own domZ; profile coverage
  >= 0.4 / 0.5). They reproduce 7,869/7,875 caller member hits and 7,616/7,632 rejected-candidate hits.
- Size: TXSScan small = T1SS, T5a/b/c; RM family small (1-3 defined proteins; its models list alternative profiles as
  separate components); DefenseFinder small when the model lists <= 4 mandatory + accessory genes.
- Family map: Pfam families overlapping a selected caller hit by >= 50% on >= 3 proteins and >= 20% of the profile's
  proteins (DPANN rule; srvp proteins). Components per cluster by matching (one protein fills one component). Window =
  span +/- (inter_gene_max_space + 1). Gene order: slot gene against the nearest hit gene, compared with called systems.
