ANSWER IN EIGHT LINES
1. N_eff (effective number of independent genomic contexts) does NOT replace the genus count. Predeclared selection raw
   W50 t2, even DH vs genus: summed -0.003 [-0.018, +0.012], F1 +.006/-.016/-.017, ribosomal 1,686 / 1,437, Parv 114/106:
   TXSScan and CRISPR-Cas lose more than 0.01. None of 48 configurations (raw/gc x W 10-100 x t 1.5-3 x noise z 0/2) met the
   three odd conditions; CRISPR-Cas F1 sits below the genus run in every one.
2. The context is informative and the failure is in the principle of reading descent from a +-W window. Shuffled contexts
   reproduce the every-genome-its-own-lineage unit (DF -0.093, CCT -0.091); real contexts recover +0.100 / +0.081 of that.
   Context cosine of two instances of one pair falls from .76 (same species) to .48 / .28 / .17 / .11 / .08 (genus / family /
   order / class / other phylum; raw W25) and stays 15x the floor mean across phyla (10+ null s.e. once pooled over 20
   genomes), because inside a conserved multi-gene unit the window is the unit. One cosine mixes shared descent with unit
   size: the ribosomal superoperon gets N_eff ~1-3 in every genome (t3, W10: 0-3 of 848 odd runs recovered) while a pair in
   50 genomes of one genus gets N_eff ~2.2 (passes t2; genus L = 1). A noise model (z = 2) and windows outside the pass-1
   locus (post hoc) do not repair it; the CRISPR-Cas loss sits on two-genus systems with shared flanks and short contigs.
3. Test 2 (embedding-only kernel, same-context list exclusion + per-instance N_eff) fails: DH -0.173 [-0.203, -0.144] vs
   round 2 with the genus, F1 -.087/-.215/-.060, 0 of 1,686 ribosomal runs recovered (support touches 5.4% of ribosomal genes,
   round 2 97.3%).
4. Test 3 is positive: label-free variety cuts replace module labels and beat them. A boundary's cohesion = mean pairwise
   cosine of the neighbours (strand-oriented, facing the boundary) of each gene's homologs, min over the two genes; cut where
   it falls under theta. Genus token landscape, LFvar theta 0.05 vs module-cut DH: +0.065 [+0.050, +0.079], F1 +.050/+.081/
   +.010, ribosomal 1,686 / 1,456, Parv 114/108. Round-2 embedding landscape (support still counts genera) read with kNN
   homologs, so with no families and no modules: LFvar 0.2 vs its module DH +0.057 [+0.041, +0.073], TXSScan F1 +0.101; vs
   token DH +0.097, F1 +.050/+.053/-.025, ribosomal 1,228.
5. The predeclared taxonomy-free, label-free token method (N_eff landscape + LFvar 0.05) against the token engine's adopted
   output (genus + module DH): +0.059 [+0.040, +0.079], F1 .789/.614/.477 (+.045/+.066/-.002), ribosomal 1,686 / 1,507, Parv
   114/106: every "matches" bar, with the summed-excess interval above 0. Under the same reading, N_eff vs genus is -0.005
   [-0.019, +0.009], F1 -.005/-.015/-.013: the cut supplies the gain, the unit still costs TXSScan and CRISPR-Cas.
6. Caveat on every cut gain: more, smaller loci (808k vs 650k; mean 4.6 vs 5.9 genes) raise the random-placement control
   F1 too (+.028/+.039/+.035). Excess gains are DF +0.023, TXSS +0.042, CRISPR-Cas -0.025 (its F1 gain is all control);
   boundary precision rises (DF .75 -> 1.0, TXSS .44 -> .67). Continuity cuts give larger F1 gains (TXSS +0.16) and resolve
   fewer ribosomal runs (1,310-1,376).
7. Direct statement: the method runs with NO module labels (better than with them; on embeddings also with no families).
   It runs with NO lineage array only on the token route, where N_eff + label-free cuts match the genus+module output; N_eff
   does not replace the genus like for like, and the embedding-only route does not run without a lineage array (Test 2).
   Best taxonomy-free output found (post hoc): lineagefree emb_hom g0.75 groups + LFvar 0.05, +0.063 vs token DH, F1
   +.051/+.072/+.006, ribosomal 1,460.
8. Cost: noise floor + N_eff for 813k candidate pairs (6.1M instances) and three nulls at four W in ~40 s on 24 CPUs after

1. THE STATISTIC
x_g      zca10 unit vector of gene g (uid-level soft-ZCA alpha 0.1 of ESM++ small means, as softrec), 960-d.
Context  instance (a, b) on contig c, lo = min, hi = max: window = genes [lo - W, lo - 1] and [hi + 1, hi + W] inside c (the
         instance and the genes between excluded). S = sum of x over the window (variant gc: x - mu_genome); n_ctx = window
         size; B = S / ||S|| (undefined for n_ctx = 0: 0.54% of instances).
Genome   B_g = unit(sum of B over the pair's instances in genome g); g is informative if one instance has n_ctx >= 1.
Pooling  k informative genomes: c_bar = (||sum_g B_g||^2 - k) / (k (k - 1)) = mean pairwise cosine; O(k d) per pair.
Noise    unrelated contexts: cos(B, B') with mean c0, sd sd0 over 2,000,000 random pairs of observed instances in different
         genomes; the mean of k(k-1)/2 such cosines has null sd sd0 sqrt(2 / (k (k - 1))).
           raw: c0 .003/.005/.006/.007, sd .064/.079/.088/.094 (W 10/25/50/100); c0 rises with window size (n_ctx 1-2: .0004;
                51-200: .016; sd .05 -> .13), the per-genome composition component (same-genome gene cosine .0148 vs .0003).
           gc:  c0 .0002-.0003, sd .051-.057, flat in n_ctx (within .0012).
         rho = clip((c_bar - c0) / (1 - c0), 0, 1)            (main);  rho_z: subtract z = 2 null sd from c_bar (amendment_1).
N_eff    G / (1 + (G - 1) rho), G = genomes carrying the pair. Kish design effect under equicorrelation; for clusters of identical
         contexts with nothing shared between clusters it equals G^2 / sum n_c^2, the inverse Simpson index (order-2
         similarity-sensitive diversity with a linear kernel). k < 2 -> rho = 0 (3,907 of 813,477 observed candidate pairs).
Engine   rule P <= 1e-e & N_eff >= t (engine selection of e: FDR <= 0.01, top 12, most genes); landscape value 2 N_eff / t,
         floored at 2; Louvain weights J log2(max(value, 2)); readings unchanged (R1; DH with Louvain 0.3, trimming 0.5).
         Computed for pairs with G >= 2 and P <= 1e-2 in the observed order and three gene-order nulls (null instances carry the
         real contexts of their positions). No taxonomy column is read (recurrence_lf.Universe, one unit per genome).
Contig truncation: median n_ctx 16 / 27 / 30 / 30 at W 10 / 25 / 50 / 100; full windows 38% / 19% / 10% / 5% of instances.

3. DIAGNOSIS: CONTEXT VECTOR OR PRINCIPLE (evaluation; 200,000 instance pairs of one token pair per relation; job 88446)
  mean cos(B, B')    same species  genus   family  order   class   other phylum   (floor sd)

  - Noise is not the limit: the floor sd (0.05-0.09) is far below every relation's mean, and the z = 2 noise model changes
    CRISPR-Cas F1 by under 0.015. W is not the limit: the curve flattens beyond W25 (contigs truncate windows; median n_ctx 30
    at W50 and W100), and every W fails. Contig length matters for CRISPR-Cas (above) but long-contig instances alone would
    still lose TXSScan at W50 (-0.017).
  - The principle: similarity of flanks measures shared descent and the extent of the conserved unit at once. Across phyla
    the cosine stays at .06-.10 (about 1 single-pair floor sd, 12-60x the floor mean, 10+ null s.e. for a pooled mean over
    20 genomes): conserved neighbourhoods extend past the pair, so the window holds
    the rest of the unit in every lineage. The design effect then discounts large conserved units as if inherited (ribosomal
    superoperon; t3 loses every run) and counts deep pairs within one genus as ~2 independent contexts (rho ~0.45 within a
    genus caps N_eff at ~2.2 for any G). No threshold separates the two, because they occupy the same range of rho.
  - Moving the window outside the pass-1 locus (post hoc, 12 configurations) removes the relatedness signal entirely (DF
    -0.06 to -0.08, CCT -0.08 to -0.09 vs genus = the genome unit; ribosomal 314-777 odd).
  - Expectations (predeclared): E1 holds (raw c0 .003 -> .007 with W; gc within .0003 of 0); E2 holds (median N_eff / G
    .506 / .525 / .535 at W 25 / 50 / 100, raw); E3 holds in its verdict (gc fails on CRISPR-Cas), not in its mechanism (gc
    W50 t2 admits 162k pairs, the genus 163k); E4 holds (raw selected, fails); E5 holds (shuffled = genome unit; refuter R2 did
    not fire); E6 refuted (member vectors lose 0.05-0.11 summed and the most DefenseFinder F1); E7 holds (partial .573);
    E8 holds (variety cuts resolve fewer runs than module cuts, 1,456 vs 1,512; continuity behaves like profile units).
    Refuter R1 fired: no (variant, W) at t 2 meets the even replacement criterion.
  - Coarse relatedness (what the genus and the lineagefree groups supply, at family-like scale) is weakly carried by flanks:
    at family level the context cosine is .17-.30, at genus .30-.51, so 'one context' is effectively 'one genus or closer'.

  gene-pair floor (.0003); kappa(k) = min over genes k and k+1 (NaN when < 2 homologs with a neighbour: no cut). Homolog set =

  Family aggregates make it O(N d). Continuity (cont): Jaccard of the genome sets of the best pairs spanning k and k+1.

CAVEATS
- Test 1's selected unit fails on the F1 rule by 0.006-0.007 per caller; the 'matches' bars it meets are the coordinator's
  looser bars, not the replacement criterion.
- The cut gains include more, smaller loci; the scorer's best-locus F1 rises with locus count, which the control F1 tracks
  (excess numbers are net of it). CRISPR-Cas excess falls under every label-free cut.
- Theta for the cuts came from a predeclared ladder chosen before any cohesion value was seen; the selected theta sits at the
  ladder's low edge (0.05) on token landscapes. Lower thetas were not run.
- Ribosomal 'resolved' for the round-2 embedding landscape is below 1,400 for its own DH (1,263) and for every cut.
- The member_raw_cosine row of evaluate_decay.json compared unaligned genes and is void (fixed in evaluate.py, not rerun;
  the row is not used here).
- Test 2 uses 128-d random projections of the contexts (per-instance pooling budget); Test 1 uses the full 960-d vectors.
- Exterior-window and post hoc cut landscapes are labelled; none enters a predeclared verdict.
