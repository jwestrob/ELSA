WS_CALIB: WHAT THE SIMILARITY BEYOND THE MMSEQS2 FAMILY IS, AND WHETHER WHITENED ESM++ SMALL SIMILARITY CARRIES
STATISTICS (DPANN, 3,564 genomes, 2026-11-13)
=====================================================================================================================
Whitened ESM++ small vectors (zca10). Families = rescue3 MMseqs2 tokens. Odd genome_code = fitting, even = scoring;
2,000-draw query bootstrap (seed 17). Predeclared in predeclared.json (02:12 UTC) after one reproduction; items marked
POST HOC were designed after the numbers they follow. Stored line reproduced exactly first (repro.json): per-genome
maximum in genomes lacking the family >= 0.30 / 0.32 / 0.40 / 0.45 / 0.50 / 0.60 = .399 / .352 / .202 / .139 / .095 /
.042 (stored .40 / 35% / .20 / 14% / .095 / 4.2%).

ANSWER IN EIGHT LINES
1. The similarity beyond the family is mostly homology that MMseqs2 families split. Share of pairs with positive homology
   evidence (Smith-Waterman E <= 1e-3, shared Pfam family or clan, CDD superfamily, or family-profile link), random
   queries: non-family neighbours at rank 1-10 .676 [.668,.685], 11-100 .587, 101-1,000 .427; per-genome best matches in
   genomes LACKING the family at cosine [.20,.32) .326, [.32,.45) .518, [.45,.60) .608, >= .60 .764; length-matched
   random pairs .015-.018. Where both proteins carry annotation: .953 / .883 / .712 and .577 / .859 / .938 / .975.
2. The split is mechanical. 59% of rank 1-10 non-family neighbours align at E <= 1e-3 (median identity .42; 87% of
   these alignments cover >= 50% of both proteins: full-length homologs that set-cover clustering placed under different
   representatives); at [.32,.45) 28% align and 48% of those fail the 50% bidirectional coverage rule (length ratio .70).
   Over all query x genome cells of random queries, 28% of best matches align to the query; 21% hold its family.
3. Same kind, different origin is a small second component: among pairs with no homology evidence, same physical kind
   (TM class, length, composition) is 2.0-2.4x the length-matched rate and both-TM 3-4x; same kind with contradicting
   annotation is .012-.083 of a band. Unexplained is .23-.59 of a band and almost all of it has an unannotated side
   ([.32,.45): unexplained .452, of which .400 unannotated side, .052 different domains and different kind).
4. The Cas pattern is the general rule. Every curated role with >= 30 queries (28; 23 non-Cas) has >= 54x more same-role
   genes of other families in its top-1,000 non-family neighbours than matched controls (per list: CRISPRCasTyper 233
   vs 0.69, DefenseFinder 103 vs 0.012, TXSScan 55 vs 0.076); the 20 largest Pfam clans 22-4,300x. H1, H2, H4 supported;
   H3 not supported by its predeclared letter (kind excess unconditional .000-.032).
5. Predeclared Q2: as detectors of "this genome holds a member of the query's MMseqs2 family" no score is clearly
   better than raw cosine and none works at low FPR: recall at FPR 1e-3 raw .026, z .021, extreme-value p .025-.027,
   CSLS .014, reciprocal-first .026, genome-centred .025, logistic .028, boosted trees .071 (+.046 [+.036,+.055]; bar
   .05). The negatives' top tail is homologs of other families (line 1). Singletons' nearest relatives: .001-.004.
6. POST HOC, cells labelled by their best match (homolog by alignment or annotation vs annotated, unrelated, unalignable):
   raw .259, within-genome z .324 (+.065 [+.046,+.084]) and best-minus-second gap .319 are the label-free gains;
   extreme-value p loses .09, CSLS hub correction loses .16 (hubs are dense real families), reciprocity and genome
   centring add <= .012; learned combinations add .10 (logistic) and .15 (boosted trees). Alignable homologs: raw .377,
   z .467.
7. Whitened ESM++ small similarity does NOT carry an E-value: per-query x per-genome extreme-value p-values run 8x / 42x
   over nominal at 1e-3 / 1e-4 on genome-level clean negatives (predeclared refuter fires) and 3x / 11x on the cleanest
   pair-level negatives; the empirical tail is 8-70x conservative. A fixed threshold works instead: z >= 10.6 / 13.7 /
   16.0 (cosine >= .504 / .649 / .746) gives FPR 1e-2 / 1e-3 / 1e-4 against clean non-homologs, equal on odd and even
   genomes, and z keeps 11 of 12 query strata within 10x of 1e-3 (raw cosine 9-10 of 12; long TM proteins worst).
8. For the method: treat beyond-family similarity as coarser homology evidence, never as the null; estimate chance by
   shuffling gene order (as the engine does), never by family absence; use z (or cosine) at a fixed threshold, skip hub
   correction and per-query tail fits. Handover (POST HOC): z-score table for every sequence x genome at z >= 10.5,
   $RD/calib/table (2.25 billion cells, 15.7 GB), 12.0 min on one A5000 for all sequences.

=====================================================================================================================
METHOD
Sample (predeclared; work/prep/queries.npz): R = the 10,000 softrec3 calibration queries (uniform over unique
  sequences, seed 17; every overall estimate uses R), S = 3,000 more of length < 100 aa, N = 3,000 rescue3 singletons with
  a Pfam hit, Y = 1,000 curated member sequences per caller, Yc = one control per Y query (no curated membership, same
  family-size bin and length tertile). 22,000 queries.
GPU (job 88065, RTX A5000, 86 s): for every query x genome the top-8 cosines and best gene, mean and sd over the genome,
  best genome-centred cosine, max_t [2 cos - r_k(t)] for k = 10 / 50 (r_k from the stored HNSW top-300), reciprocal best
  match in the query's genome; per query the histograms of raw cosines and within-genome z over all 3,585,303 genes and
  the exact top-2,001 unique sequences. Check: the per-genome maximum equals softrec3's CPU omax within 1e-3 in 100% of
  the 35.6 M cells of the 10,000 shared queries (max |d| 5.2e-4), same best gene in 99.90%.
Evidence lines (evaluation only; Sharur DPANN seal 78c68b6b exported read-only): MMseqs2 align (Smith-Waterman, mode 3)
  of explicit pairs against the 2,547,528-sequence uniqdb, E-values at that database size; Pfam families and Pfam 39
  clans (from Pfam-A.seed); CDD hits (E <= 0.01) mapped to cl superfamilies; family-level HHsearch-Pfam / HHblits-PDB100
  hits (prob >= 90, >= 30 columns; 5,090 / 5,344 Pfam-dark families) linked through Pfam family, clan or PDB entry;
  curated roles from system_proteins; physical kind from sequence (Kyte-Doolittle 19-residue TM heuristic, length,
  amino-acid composition). Partition: HOM = any homology line; ROLE; KIND = same TM class AND length ratio >= .67 AND
  composition L1 <= .404 (uniform-pair p25); each split by annotation status.
Q1 sets: rank bands among non-family neighbours (10 per band per query) and family-absent per-genome best matches by
  cosine band (5 per band per query); baselines: 20 uniform random genes per query and one length-matched (+-10%) random
  gene per neighbour. 1,038,728 neighbour pairs, 2,453,555 unique aligned pairs (15 s).
Q2 detectors D1-D12 as predeclared; tail windows chosen on odd ((1e-2, 1e-3) for both extreme-value scores);
  combinations fitted on 2 M odd cells of R. Labels: FAM (genome holds the family), CLAN (genome holds a protein sharing
  a Pfam family or clan), genome-level clean negatives (no shared family, clan or CDD superfamily anywhere in the genome).
Q2b (POST HOC): every R cell labelled by its best raw match (30.6 M explicit alignments, 3.7 min): HOM = alignable at
  E <= 1e-3 or shared Pfam / clan / CDD / profile link (41.5% of cells; alignable 28.2%); NEGC = both annotated, nothing
  shared, E > 1 (17.8%); unknown 40.7%.

The direct statement: whitened ESM++ small similarity does not support an extreme-value E-value of the BLAST kind.
Per-query tails are not exponential beyond the 1e-3 quantile, homolog contamination of each query's own distribution
is large (families of up to 7,712 genes), and every per-query normalisation tried (EV fits, empirical tail, CSLS) ranks
worse than the raw score because cross-query differences in scale are mostly real (dense families). A global threshold
on the within-genome z-score behaves like a calibrated cut: the same value gives the same FPR on independent genome
halves and across most query strata.

=====================================================================================================================
WHAT A METHOD SHOULD DO WITH THE BEYOND-FAMILY SIMILARITY
- Count it as evidence. Above cosine .32 a per-genome best match outside the query's MMseqs2 family is a
  homolog in most cases where annotation can decide (.86-.98); .47-.90 of those pairs align outright. Suppressing it as
  noise throws away the coarser homology classes (curated roles, Pfam clans) that the whitened space recovers and that
  the Cas lists already showed.
- Never build the null from family absence. "Genome lacks the family" is a poor negative set (FAM negatives hold
  homologs in their top tail; every detector scores <= .07 recall at FPR 1e-3 against them). The engine's gene-order
  shuffle remains the right null for recurrence; for pairwise thresholds use NEGC-type clean negatives.
- Use a fixed z (or cosine) threshold per stated error rate: z 10.6 / 13.7 / 16.0 for 1e-2 / 1e-3 / 1e-4 per cell. Skip
  hub correction (CSLS), per-query extreme-value p-values and reciprocity filters; a genome-centred term and the gap
  carry the remaining label-free gain.
- Expect a coarse tier: below z ~10 the matches mix homology, same-kind resemblance (TM with TM) and dark proteins with
  no evidence either way; a two-grain statistic (family-level and clan-level support counted separately) fits these
  data better than one floor.

=====================================================================================================================
CAVEATS
- "Homology evidence" is a lower bound: 40% of pairs have an unannotated side, and alignment misses distant homologs.
  The evaluable-pair rates (both annotated) are an upper-side view biased toward well-annotated families.
- The physical-kind lines are heuristics (Kyte-Doolittle TM, composition); no TM or signal-peptide caller output is
  stored in Sharur. Curated roles name nothing beyond what the callers emitted; no system names are assigned here.
- Q2 labels FAM / CLAN are genome-level; detectors score the best match of the genome. CLAN positives include genomes
  whose same-clan protein is not the best match.
- Q2b, the per-stratum views, the conditional kind analysis and all thresholds are POST HOC. The combination models use
  annotation-derived labels for training; applying them needs no annotation, but they are fitted models.
- The boosted-tree score uses query length and hubness; part of its gain is query-level prior, not cell evidence.
- Q1 fine bins mix queries (5 cells per band per query), hence the non-monotone .45-.50 bin.
- Process: Q2 job 88119 lost to repeated npz reads, 88152 to an index bug (no number read either time except the odd
  window choice, which is a fitting step); 88202 cancelled seconds after start (submitted at exactly 400 account CPUs);
  GPU job 88450 cancelled while pending (another workstream held the gpu slot). q1_extra ran 16 min single-threaded on
  the login node (bootstrap weights redrawn per call).
