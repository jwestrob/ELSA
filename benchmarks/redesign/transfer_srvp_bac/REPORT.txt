6. Differences from the DPANN package, all forced by the data
  - Source check: the Sharur seal is structural (no file digest), so the copy is checked against the Mac file's
    SHA-256 taken before transfer, plus the seal's 29 table row counts.
  - Strand check uses the Prodigal calls that produced the database (no independent re-call exists).
  - Taxonomy comes from the Sharur bins string (GTDB-Tk r226) for every genome; the r232 accession lookup is kept
    and matches nothing (unpublished MAGs).
  - Gold adds two curated locus callers absent from DPANN (GECCO, sharur.nitrogenase_loci) in the same format.
  - universe/gene_flags.parquet and universe/proteins.faa are added beside the DPANN files.
  - tsv2parquet.py widens lengths to UINTEGER (a 78,141-aa protein exceeds USMALLINT).
  - The rescue search runs only the rescue queries (per-query results are independent of the other queries).
  - The March ESM2-8M stage is dropped; ESM++ small is the embedding.
  - The scorer self-test on gold-as-loci and the whole-contig example were not run, because both read the curated
    calls; the scorer was tested on a synthetic package only.
