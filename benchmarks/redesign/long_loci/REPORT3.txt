FAILURE ANALYSIS
- The embedding lists add bonds on this collection but not long-locus recall: Z bonds 43.9% of boundaries (family
  bonds 39.7%) and its L1 covers 56% of genes (family L1 51%), yet Z's L1 scores +.131 against family L1's +.153
  (-.021 [-.055, +.011]): the extra bonds land as often outside the regions as inside (control .439 vs .427).
- The tau rule gives 8 here (6 on DPANN): chance co-location of z lists is higher across 1,317 phylogenetically spread
  genomes (shuffled-order FDR at 6 is .024 against .009 on DPANN).
- Synteny counting (Zs) loses .025 [-.045, -.007] against group counting (Z), most in regions of 1-5 genes (+.085 vs
  +.132). The mechanism was not diagnosed here; a discount of support among the related Streptomyces /
  Micromonospora genomes is a candidate (UNVERIFIED).
- The best single locus still covers a median one third of a region of >= 11 genes; nesting and recurrence do not
  supply the engine's wider window (transfer_spacedust: w 8 adds +.062 over w 3 for the engine on the same tokens).

CAVEATS
- A and As (the arms that met all DPANN bars most cleanly) are not tested here; the Spacedust verdict is for the
  embedding arms only.
- The kNN is exact here and HNSW on DPANN; both feed only the exclusion masks and the kNN cohesion.
- The GPU jobs ran with the partition's default host memory (12 GB for 12 CPUs); both peaked at that value and
  completed.
- Every source207 number is a development result; nothing was chosen on this collection.
