2026-11-12 13:27 UTC  Design fixed, predeclared.json written before any job was submitted.
  Question: token-free recurrence from per-gene ranked neighbour lists vs the token engine.
  Key design decision: lists exclude the gene's own genus. Reason: Parvarchaeum has 190 genomes (largest
  genus; 922 genera, median 2 genomes per genus); a top-50 list that may include own-genus genes would be
  consumed by same-genus copies, which can never add a lineage to L. Depth is therefore counted in genes
  outside the own genus. Consequence: G and E are computed over other-genus genomes (the engine counts
  own-genus genomes in G and E); L is unaffected. The skip-genome family run checks the engine identity.
  MMseqs2 hit lists: $RD/repair/hits (1,093,510 rescue/split query uniques) and $RD/subgenic/hits (S queries from
  356 sampled genomes + gold neighbourhoods; giants/bench sets) cover < 1.47 M of 2,547,528 unique sequences;
  no fair genome-wide list exists -> degenerate family lists are the control, as instructed.
  Family-list cost: sum over tokens of size^2 = 3.34e9 (base), 3.41e9 (rescue3): the full degenerate run is
  affordable with the same kernel.
