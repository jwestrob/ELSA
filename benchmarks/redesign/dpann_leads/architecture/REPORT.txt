BOTTOM LINE
1. The screen yields one architecture worth studying and no SUPPORTED lead. Of 175,729 labeled genes, 60 conserved
   architectures pass every control; 3 join two different domain families, 3 duplicate one family, and the rest carry
   regulator, surface, mobile-element, known-protein or hit-less extensions (counts below).
2. Best lead, WEAK: L1, a 531-559 aa single chain that fuses a MobA/LicC-type nucleotidyltransferase (N-terminal,
   NTP_transf_3) to an APH/choline-kinase-type kinase (C-terminal): 14 genes, 14 genomes, 5 genera, 3 orders. Both active
   sites are intact (residues + superposition on LicC 1JYL and LicA 4R78), and an ESMFold2 co-fold places CTP + Mg in the
   NTase site and ATP in the kinase site, 28 A apart. It sits in a glycan locus. The substrate is UNVERIFIED: the LicC
   choline-binding residues are replaced, so the CDP-choline reading of the curated LicCA relative does not transfer.
   Rung 5 cannot be tested without a substrate. Embedding and alignment lists agree on all 14 (embedding-only 0/14).
3. KILLED: L2 Tmk + NTP_transf_5 fusion (33/35 genomes carry a separate dTMP kinase), L3 bicupin (fold only, no residue or
   family-structure support), L4 KamA-like (PLP lysine and Zn site absent), L5 M29 long form (13/28 already carry KO).
4. Sanity check: the screen does not flag the Thy1 (~550 aa) or HMG_CoA_synt_C (~580 aa) cases, because DPANN carriers
   already have those lengths: Thy1 carriers median 520 aa (1,806 proteins; 1,138 carry two Thy1 domains), ratio 1.06;
   HMG_CoA_synt_C carriers median 561 aa, ratio 1.04. By this screen's known-form rule both are the common DPANN form.
   (code/s0_sanity_thy1_hmgcs.py)
