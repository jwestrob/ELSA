FAILURE ANALYSIS
- The L4b tree costs excess on Spacedust at every window (WA L4b +.164 against WA L1 +.247): nested sub-nodes raise the
  random-placement control (.504 against .432) more than F1 (.667 against .679). On DPANN the same nesting is what
  resolves ribosomal runs. The two benchmarks reward opposite readings of the same tree: whole runs (Spacedust) and
  the run-sized child (DPANN).
- Wider fixed windows link more across system edges on DPANN: CRISPR-Cas F1 stays .453-.456 for W5 / W8 / W3b, below
  the bar; the adaptive test at distance 1 (smaller E) adds adjacent pairs of rare systems and lifts CRISPR-Cas to .461.
- The summed DPANN excess falls with distance (W3b .580, W5 .568, W8 .548, WA .513 at the selected theta .2; .540 at
  .1): longer loci raise the control for DefenseFinder and TXSScan.

CAVEATS
- The selected theta (.2) beat theta .1 by one odd resolved run (806 vs 805); theta .1 gives the same resolved count on
  all runs (1,594) with summed .540 and is also 7/7.
- WA's CRISPR-Cas bar holds by .001 (point estimate -.019 against -.02; interval -.029 to -.008).
- WA L4b vs the engine: -.020 is BELOW by the predeclared rule by 4e-5; the interval includes 0.
- The bond-FDR rule is new in this part (part 1 used the word-type rule); W3b separates its effect from the window's.
- Spacedust rows other than the primary are development rows; window 8 had been seen there.
