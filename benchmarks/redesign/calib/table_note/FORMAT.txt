ws_calib handover table (POST HOC; job 88624; code calib/code/gpu_table.py)
=============================================================================
What: for every unique sequence q (uid 0..2,547,527, same uid as the embedding run, subgenic/uniq.parquet and
softrec/r3/vec/meta.npz 'uq') and every genome G (genome_code 0..3,563): the best gene of G for q by whitened ESM++
small cosine (zca10, float16 products on one A5000), its cosine, and the within-genome z-score
    z = (best cosine - mean of q's cosines to all genes of G) / their standard deviation.
Stored only where z >= 10.5.

Error rate (POST HOC, softrec3 calibration queries, pair-level clean negatives = best match and query both annotated by
Pfam or CDD, sharing no Pfam family, clan, CDD superfamily or family-profile link, Smith-Waterman E > 1):
    z >= 10.57 : FPR 1e-2 per (query, genome) cell; recall of homologous best matches .553 (alignable .756)
    z >= 13.65 : FPR 1e-3;                          recall .324 (alignable .467); precision vs clean negatives .999
    z >= 15.96 : FPR 1e-4;                          recall .151 (alignable .220)
Thresholds are equal on odd and even genome halves (calib/results/q2b_thr.json). Raw cosine equivalents: .504 / .649 /
.746. "Homologous" = alignable at E <= 1e-3 or sharing Pfam family / clan / CDD superfamily / profile link; roughly 40%
of cells cannot be labelled either way, so these are rates against clean negatives, not against all non-homologs.

Files (sparse, ordered by uid, then genome_code ascending):
  counts.npy   int32[2,547,528]  entries per uid; offsets = np.r_[0, np.cumsum(counts)]
  genome.bin   int16  genome_code
  local.bin    int16  index of the best gene inside the genome (gid = meta['g_start'][genome] + local)
  cos.bin      float16 best cosine
  z.bin        float16 z-score
  table.json   run provenance, entry counts, timing
Cells of genomes that hold q's own sequence are included (cosine 1); drop them when a list should exclude self.
